# Community credits and learning record

Glitter filtering research, 23 September 2026: **Jakob, Hašan, Yan, Lawrence, Ramamoorthi and Marschner**'s [Discrete Stochastic Microfacet Models (2014)](https://research.cs.cornell.edu/stochastic-sg14/) explains the aliasing/temporal-coherence problem with tiny normal-mapped glitter. **Kneiphof and Klein**'s [Real-time Image-based Lighting of Glints (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02674v1) identifies environment lighting as a separate filtering requirement. **Thomas Deliot and Laurent Belcour**'s [2023 anisotropic-grid paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05051v1) adds a pixel-footprint counting lead; the paper title block confirms authorship. These are conceptual/abstract-level research inputs, with no copied code, assets or implemented paper algorithms. [Lessons and application](../research/materials/glitter-filtering-research.md).

Makeup raster acceleration, 23 September 2026: Nathan supplied the real authored reproduction used to expose expensive variable-softness and mirrored sampling. Existing Bun/TypeScript and Chrome tooling enabled independent byte and timing checks. The conservative pruning, exact pair reuse and task scheduling implementation are project-authored; no new community mod implementation or asset was copied. [Evidence](../research/authoring/raster-performance.md).

Irregular glitter prototype, 23 September 2026: Nathan's locally preserved makeup photographs informed varied fragment sizes/spacing and independent pale-gold flake colour over pigment. Authorship remains unresolved as recorded in the photo manifest; no photograph is redistributed or traced. Existing Three.js material/channel learning informs the pending adapter, while Bun 1.4.2, NumPy 2.5.2 and Pillow 12.3.0 support tests, spectral measurements and encoding of owned generated pixels. Fragment algorithms and study code are project-authored. See [experiment and evidence](../experiments/007-irregular-glitter/findings.md) and [current integration audit](../research/materials/glitter-quality-integration.md).

Whole-shape gestures and UV indexing, 23 September 2026: existing **Three.js 0.186.0** geometry/raycast/typed-buffer facilities and the local CDPR-derived plate enable deformation-aware editing and indexed-versus-exhaustive lookup validation. **Bun 1.4.2** runs numeric/adapter tests and **SQLite** retains authored results. This extends the dependency and asset entries below. The transform mathematics, UV hierarchy and gesture adapters are project-authored; no new community implementation was copied. See [contract and evidence](../research/authoring/shape-gesture-contract.md).

## Frameworks, tools and shared documentation

### ArchiveXL — psiberx and contributors

[Repository](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-archive-xl), inspected release 1.27.3, commit `5474e34d56112f5d8843ae863e1e72ff510957c0`.

The customization, mesh and garment extension source established exactly how CCXL registers options, clones appearance templates, routes suffixes to mesh appearances, expands material paths, and splits composite material attributes. This is the central technical foundation for avoiding the old per-combination material matrix and for the proposed single-selector preset exporter. The author's wiki also helped distinguish garment context from character-creator capabilities.

**Use:** source-grounded learning and intended runtime dependency; not a claim that ArchiveXL source has been incorporated into our implementation. [Strategy and pinned source evidence](../research/archive-xl/eye-artistry-strategy.md) record functions, limits and unresolved game checks.

The [first one-selector fixture](../experiments/005-preset-collection/README.md) additionally uses the garment hook's missing-appearance guard to preserve an exact empty Off, a named component override for cloned presets, and shared material expansion with lightweight mesh stubs. Inspecting the first-override-array assumption informed the generated empty Off structure. Its source-derived checks remain distinct from game execution.

Additional eye-resolution finding: installed ArchiveXL 1.26.3 shared-eye resources and September 16 logs, checked against source 1.27.3 at the revision above, establish dynamic material instantiation and rejection of fallback links when real resources already exist. This explains why a static app lookup misses a valid CCXL eye choice. [Saved-eye research](../research/eye-artistry/modded-eye-resolution.md) separates historical runtime evidence from current installed assets. **Use:** source/resource learning, no copied framework implementation.

### WolvenKit — the WolvenKit team and contributors

[Repository](https://github.com/WolvenKit/WolvenKit). Save-format research used commit `11720772f1e20581301b3dec88a59f7b5ee05675`; conversion experiments use installed CLI 8.17.4. Do not conflate these revisions.

Its save parser supplied format facts for our narrow read-only TypeScript appearance reader. Shader-cache structures made the cache index/disassembly investigation possible. Mesh/morph and animation conversion exposed packing, bone-map, shading-delta and float-track details, while its CLI and libraries enabled extraction, serialization, round-trip verification and animation export. Facial-setup readers provided the first map of the game's pose/corrective data.

**Use:** learning plus executed tools and referenced .NET libraries. Evidence: [save reader research](../research/eye-artistry/save-import.md), [shader investigation](../research/materials/glitter-shader-investigation.md), and [plate conversion](../experiments/004-plate-import/README.md). Animation intake is ongoing. Any distributed dependency must retain its own applicable license/notices; this record is not a license inventory.

Its appearance preprocessor and RedPackage writer also taught us how newly authored component definitions become compiled `.app` data. [Experiment 005](../experiments/005-preset-collection/README.md) verifies that actual conversion, independent handle identities, packed resources and decoded texture channels rather than relying only on our generated JSON.

[Experiment 006](../experiments/006-plate-clearance/README.md) extends the resolver lesson to read-only head morph export: the original base mesh archive is needed to recover skin weights/bones, as well as for morph import. Its packing behavior also exposes the small nonzero lighting delta produced when newly introduced morph records encode an intended zero in the shifted 10-bit format. We separately record that quantization rather than claiming exact shading preservation for those new records.

Further source study at the same commit, 23 September 2026: [`MeshTools.cs`](https://github.com/WolvenKit/WolvenKit/blob/11720772f1e20581301b3dec88a59f7b5ee05675/WolvenKit.Modkit/RED4/Tools/MeshTools.cs) normalizes native byte weights during export; [`MeshImportTools.cs`](https://github.com/WolvenKit/WolvenKit/blob/11720772f1e20581301b3dec88a59f7b5ee05675/WolvenKit.Modkit/RED4/Tools/MeshImportTools.cs) rounds incoming normalized weights back to bytes. This explained why a cut-out with effectively identical weights to the head changed by up to 0.004329 after conversion. It informed our new [native skin-byte preservation experiment](../experiments/006-plate-clearance/retain_head_weights.py), which maps bone slots by name and keeps other bytes unchanged. **Use:** source-format learning and executed conversion tools, plus local reuse of CDPR's original skin data; our transfer implementation is new code. Source inspection and installed-tool round-trip evidence remain distinct.

[`MorphTargetTools.cs`](https://github.com/WolvenKit/WolvenKit/blob/11720772f1e20581301b3dec88a59f7b5ee05675/WolvenKit.Modkit/RED4/Tools/MorphTargetTools.cs) and [`MorphTargetImportTools.cs`](https://github.com/WolvenKit/WolvenKit/blob/11720772f1e20581301b3dec88a59f7b5ee05675/WolvenKit.Modkit/RED4/Tools/MorphTargetImportTools.cs) clarified a second requirement: a morph resource embeds its own base vertex buffer. Its referenced mesh supplies the bone-name/rig mapping but does not replace that embedded skin data. A mesh-only retention trial left exported morph weights unchanged; this led us to preserve and independently verify both buffers, retaining the morph delta/mapping buffers untouched.

The same WolvenKit source revision’s `ArchiveReader.cs` supplied archive-index structure for the [saved-eye provider scan](../research/eye-artistry/modded-eye-resolution.md). CLI 8.17.4 hash extraction, serialization and XBM→PNG conversion resolved resources absent from its path dictionary. **Use:** format learning and executed tools; no reader implementation copied into the app.
