# Community credits and learning record

XF Studio builds on the discoveries, tools and creativity of the Cyberpunk modding community. This living record gives credit for what we learn, including ideas that inform an independent implementation. It also identifies tools, dependencies and assets used in our work so their contributions remain visible when preparing public acknowledgements and third-party notices before distribution.

Started 23 September 2026, with an initial backfill from existing research. This is not a claim that every earlier influence has already been recovered; add missing contributions as they are identified. Authorship of the Nexus mods below was checked against each page's **Created by** field on 23 September 2026 unless an entry says otherwise. Installed versions describe our actual research inputs, not necessarily the latest release.

**How to add or extend an entry.** Find the credited party's heading in the appropriate category and add a bullet to its existing entry; create a new heading, in alphabetical order, only for a party not yet credited. Supply the fields in the [entry checklist](#entry-checklist). Also add any unresolved authorship, provenance or reuse-permission gap to [Open provenance follow-ups](#open-provenance-follow-ups). Do not add dated changelog paragraphs above this introduction or at the end of the file.

**Relationship types.** Each entry distinguishes:

- **Learning** — source, documentation, resource, image or conversation study that informs an independent, project-authored implementation.
- **Tool/dependency use** — an executed tool, runtime, library or framework.
- **Code adaptation** — upstream implementation structure actually adapted into our code; its licence and notices must travel with it.
- **Asset use** — a third-party asset used by us. *Private local reference* means ignored, hash-verified local copies for Nathan's studio only, never tracked in Git or released.

Credit never grants reuse or redistribution permission. Unless an entry records otherwise, no third-party code, game or mod payload, image, shader, or game-derived binary entered Git or any release, and offline inspection is not proof of in-game rendering or runtime loading.

## Game developer

### CD PROJEKT RED

- **Credit:** CD PROJEKT RED, developer of Cyberpunk 2077. Installed game version 2.31 unless stated.
- **Relationship:** private game-resource learning (resources, compiled shaders, rigs, animation graphs) and private local reference use of original game art, rigs, facial setups, animation and materials. These underpin our local preview and experiments. They remain game-derived references, not original XF Studio or legacy Eye Artistry art. Extracted meshes, morphs, textures, shaders, disassembly, bakes and renders stay ignored and local; attribution grants no redistribution right, and source inspection is not proof of runtime appearance or loading.

**What we learned / used for**

- **Technical-art rationale:** CDPR's explanation in [A World Full of Substance](https://magazine.substance3d.com/cyberpunk-2077-a-world-full-of-substance/) contributed the rationale for shared surfaces, masks and a calibrated external preview; see the [multilayered makeup assessment](../research/materials/multilayered-makeup-assessment.md).
- **Head geometry and skin data:** the expanded plate was cut from CDPR head geometry; see [Nathan (axefrog)](#nathan-axefrog). [Native skin-byte preservation](../experiments/006-plate-clearance/retain_head_weights.py) locally reuses CDPR's original skin data (project-authored transfer code). The [native plate bootstrap](../experiments/012-native-plate-bootstrap/README.md) exported the installed female head as an offline neutral geometry source, not clearance, game appearance or distribution permission. The local CDPR-derived plate and head enabled deformation-aware [whole-shape gestures](../research/authoring/shape-gesture-contract.md) and the [projected tangent](../research/authoring/projected-tangent-controls.md) raycast parity check. Plate clearance studies use this geometry and its morphs only as ignored local inputs (see [Blender](#blender) and [SciPy and NumPy](#scipy-and-numpy)).
- **Native eye assembly (25 September):** the installed female eye mesh and default UI idle are private source data for the [57-joint offline comparison](../experiments/015-native-eye-assembly/README.md), teaching distinct eye versus pupil motion. The [native-eye intake gate](../research/eye-artistry/native-eye-preview-intake-gate.md) privately stages the installed morph resource after project-authored checks.
- **Eye/head contacts and h091:** the [finite native eye/head follow-up](../experiments/015-native-eye-assembly/finite-clearance.md) found baseline eye, lash and wetness intersections, plus additional eye/head contacts on the saved five-morph head at selected idle phases; its opaque ray proxy does not establish REDengine material or alpha behaviour. The [visual inspection](../experiments/015-native-eye-assembly/visual-inspection.md) used the eye mesh's embedded material links, the `eyelashes_wa_01.mi` strand-alpha texture and the `eyeshadow_base.mi` wetness-slot link to make exposed regions of the saved blink visible. The [source-graph trace](../experiments/015-native-eye-assembly/source-graph-trace.md) corrected that study: head/eye entity and morph resources reveal a separate eye `h091` customization target with coordinated head/eye joint matrices, which the prior render omitted; the face-rig entity and animation graph link to the female UI face anim set and close-up states, while live state/morph activation remains unproved. The [h091-aware comparison](../experiments/015-native-eye-assembly/h091-comparison.md) showed that applying the native eye, lash and wetness `h091` target under the pinned offline idle removes the large upper-eye islands in the saved blink but leaves a narrower lower strip; the REDengine morph-bind blend and live graph policy remain unproved.
- **Native eye preview core and brown gradient:** [Experiment 013](../experiments/013-native-preview-core/README.md) used the installed female eye mesh, six fixed vanilla head/eye XBMs and mesh/material resources for the `gradient_brown` eye binding, linked brown gradient, tiled UVs and texture channel metadata; the brown profile and game shader remain unadapted. The [Experiment 014 input probe](../experiments/014-native-eye-gradient/README.md) showed `eye_brown.gradient`, `brown_eye_gradient.mi` and `eye_gradient.mt` carry three ordered colour stops, exact instance-to-template/profile links and iris/refraction/mask parameters, so the plan neither treats the grey-blue base diffuse as the complete brown eye nor treats the stops as a proved renderer lookup. The [compiled brown-eye and gaze trace](../experiments/014-native-eye-gradient/compiled-shader-and-gaze.md) found the selected `eye_gradient` G-buffer program uses iris-mask red for a gradient-atlas lookup, alpha to blend with albedo, and roughness red times its material scale; all 57 eye-mesh bone names, including both eye joints under `Head`, are present in the installed sibling facial rig. Runtime gradient-row interpolation and the gaze transform remain open gates.
- **Eye and lip optics:** the [eye/lip optics audit](../research/eye-artistry/eye-lip-optics-audit.md) checked `eye.mt`, `skin.mt` and compiled shader programs; the eye roughness shader samples **red**, correcting our proposed green-channel mapping and supplying the red-channel/scale evidence for the [opt-in roughness study](../research/eye-artistry/eye-roughness-preview.md). The [isolated eye/lip lighting study](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/render-fidelity-study-2026-09-24.md) found uniform 0.85 skin roughness removes the preview's bright lip line while flat normals do not, and eye R/G differences are visually tiny at one angle. The [D05/Arkhe source-map A/B](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/render-fidelity-source-maps-2026-09-24.md) relied on native preview UV0/triangle correspondence and the game's packed RG normal and R/B roughness inputs. The [light-component and mesh-ownership follow-up](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/render-fidelity-study-2026-09-24.md#light-components-and-mesh-ownership--25-september-2026) used head geometry and privately staged base D05 maps and showed upper and lower lips share the head mesh, so mesh ownership alone cannot gate a lip blur. The [lip ownership gate](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/render-fidelity-ownership-gate-2026-09-25.md) showed from triangle and UV connectivity that visible lips share the large face island; smaller islands and atlas seams do not supply validated lip ownership.
- **Skin chain and wrinkles:** the [saved skin and mouth source trace](../research/eye-artistry/saved-skin-resource-chain.md) resolved the base skin/teeth chain; the [saved-skin shader and source-order audit](../research/eye-artistry/saved-skin-shader-and-winner.md) took roughness-R/B and packed-normal-RG findings from the compiled 2.31 skin shader and material registers. The [reproducible wrinkle probe](../research/animation/brow-idle-gap.md#25-september-reproducible-processed-wrinkle-probe)'s saved-head material trace showed inherited stretch/squash wrinkle normal inputs exist and the inspected `skin` variant samples them, but no index-to-mask or runtime binding is proved.
- **Hair, brow and lash shading:** the installed hair shader cache revealed separate alpha-accumulation and base-colour passes, without establishing the Soft Natural lash's exact runtime bindings or tint ([lash follow-up](../research/eye-artistry/lash-material-followup.md)). The compiled shader independently established the combined-alpha, gradient and normal paths for the saved brow ([brow diagnostic](../research/eye-artistry/brow-lash-preview-diagnostic.md)).
- **Decal material families:** installed resources independently checked wiki claims for the [REDengine glint feasibility study](../research/materials/redengine-glint-feasibility.md). The `mesh_decal_blendable` template and compiled shader informed the coverage-masked, single-lobe hypothesis in the [Glossy feasibility study](../research/materials/glossy-decal-feasibility.md) and taught that the stock pass offers only one roughness-controlled surface reflection for a real Studio Glossy recipe ([Experiment 008](../experiments/008-glossy-decal/README.md); clearcoat unmapped). The `mesh_decal` squared-alpha destination arithmetic led to project-authored premultiplied mips in the [flat preset mip study](../research/materials/flat-preset-mip-filtering.md). `mesh_decal` and `mesh_decal_emissive_subsurface` supplied stock-pass hypotheses for the [Glitter game-material comparison](../experiments/009-glitter-game-fixture/README.md) and the hash-pinned templates for the [recipe-driven stock-material check](../experiments/009-glitter-game-fixture/recipe-driven-boundary.md). The [particle-decal audit](../experiments/009-glitter-game-fixture/particle-decal-audit.md) showed the `mesh_decal_particles` skinned highlight pass is not a facet-glint evaluator. The `mesh_decal` contract underlies the [recipe-driven Shimmer fixture](../experiments/010-shimmer-game-adapter/README.md) and the [private Shimmer plate comparison](../experiments/011-shimmer-plate-comparison/README.md). The `mesh_decal_gradientmap_recolor_blendable` skinned pass has a masked view-angle Fresnel RGB addition, suggesting a narrow two-tone candidate rather than the Studio's broader thin-film effect ([Colour-shifting feasibility audit](../research/materials/colour-shift-game-feasibility.md)). The [glitter implementation contract](../research/materials/glitter-implementation-contract.md) reuses these pinned decal findings without distributing extracted assets.
- **Jewellery material:** the game's brushed-silver earring material, linked by a PRC recolour archive, is CD PROJEKT RED source, not a community-authored shader ([PRC stud material trace](../research/jewellery/prc-catalog-audit.md#stud-second-chunk-material-resolution-24-september)).

### ArchiveXL

- **Credit:** psiberx and contributors. [Repository](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-archive-xl).
- **Versions:** source release 1.27.3 at commit `5474e34d56112f5d8843ae863e1e72ff510957c0` ([release page](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-archive-xl/releases/tag/v1.27.3)); immutable `v1.26.3` source at [commit `d4beb964`](https://github.com/psiberx/cp2077-archive-xl/blob/d4beb964ce61de722e083f1fb976bc31e4cb9f49/src/App/Archives/ArchiveService.cpp#L95-L120); installed 1.26.3 with logs from 16 September 2026.
- **Relationship:** source-grounded learning and intended runtime dependency. ArchiveXL source has not been incorporated into our implementation. The official 1.27.3 release ZIP is privately installed for diagnostic testing only.

**What we learned / used for**

- **Core foundation:** the customization, mesh and garment extension source established exactly how CCXL registers options, clones appearance templates, routes suffixes to mesh appearances, expands material paths and splits composite material attributes. This is the central technical foundation for avoiding the old per-combination material matrix and for the single-selector preset exporter. The author's wiki also helped distinguish garment context from character-creator capabilities. [Strategy and pinned source evidence](../research/archive-xl/eye-artistry-strategy.md) record functions, limits and unresolved game checks.
- **One-selector fixture:** the [first fixture](../experiments/005-preset-collection/README.md) uses the garment hook's missing-appearance guard to preserve an exact empty Off, a named component override for cloned presets, and shared material expansion with lightweight mesh stubs; inspecting the first-override-array assumption informed the generated empty Off structure. The [private Shimmer plate comparison](../experiments/011-shimmer-plate-comparison/README.md) reuses this registration pattern while checking its own explicit appearance graph. Source-derived checks remain distinct from game execution.
- **Package boundary:** the [local collection package boundary](../research/authoring/local-package-build.md) carries the same rules into a separately verified private package candidate; no framework code copied and no in-game registration established.
- **Saved-eye resolution:** installed 1.26.3 shared-eye resources and the 16 September logs, checked against 1.27.3 source, establish dynamic material instantiation and rejection of fallback links when real resources already exist, explaining why a static app lookup misses a valid CCXL eye choice ([saved-eye research](../research/eye-artistry/modded-eye-resolution.md)). ArchiveXL's scoped eye links also identified the native mesh for the [eye adapter plan](../research/eye-artistry/eye-preview-adapter-plan.md).
- **Creator catalog:** a loose `.xl` registration and an anonymous `eyes_color` appearance overlay are only part of the selector graph; dynamic app redirects and resource patches must be resolved before matching a saved app hash ([read-only catalog prototype](../research/character-customization/catalog-prototype.md), with [nutboy](#nutboy)'s mod as the concrete case). The dynamic app follow-up applied the installed 1.26.3 app-path fix and transitive `player_customization.app` scope, yielding one exact source-derived match for Nathan's saved `eye_16_diffuse` app hash (read-only resolver, not a claim about effective archive winners).
- **Lash profile overlap:** a dated 1.26.3 log showed the base-colour patch adding `brown_liquorice` to icxrus's custom female lash mesh, while the registered customization and dynamic material path explain why the source mesh did not contain the saved colour appearance literally ([saved lash profile overlap](../research/eye-artistry/brown-liquorice-profile-overlap.md)); historical assembly evidence, not proof of the selected `.hp` bytes or rendered colour. At the [loader-source boundary](../research/eye-artistry/brown-liquorice-profile-overlap.md#loader-source-boundary-25-september-2026), `v1.26.3` inserts extra `Mod` groups before non-mod groups in the engine depot — architecture evidence, not proof of the native lookup result or of Nathan's effective archive mount.
- **Brow assembly log:** a captured 16 September log confirms Arkhe's style-18 appearance expansion; see [Arkhe](#arkhe).
- **Diagnostic framework gate (25 September):** the 1.27.3 release page and tagged README helped choose a stable diagnostic target and identify which compatibility claims cover game 2.31 ([framework gate](toolchain.md#runtime-evidence-and-current-stable-releases)). The [profile-only framework preparation](../research/authoring/framework-diagnostic-profile-2026-09-25.md) installed the complete official release ZIP as a private side-by-side MO2 mod; it retains its own LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES files. The [first makeup runtime preflight](../research/authoring/first-makeup-runtime-preflight-2026-09-25.md) rechecked it byte-for-byte. Inspect the package's licence and notices if XF Studio ever bundles it.

## Modding tools

### Cyberpunk Blender Add-on (IO Suite)

- **Credit:** its authors and the RED Modding maintainers. Credit the [upstream author list](https://github.com/WolvenKit/Cyberpunk-Blender-add-on/blob/7a4ee793c36d9615946fe87ec9d42cde7568021d/i_scene_cp77_gltf/__init__.py) and contributors collectively. **Individual authorship of the facial solver, and the specific authors of the `eye.py`/`nodes.py` changes, remain unresolved.**
- **Source:** [repository](https://github.com/WolvenKit/Cyberpunk-Blender-add-on), intake commit `7a4ee793c36d9615946fe87ec9d42cde7568021d` ([pinned source](https://github.com/WolvenKit/Cyberpunk-Blender-add-on/tree/7a4ee793c36d9615946fe87ec9d42cde7568021d)), inspected 23 September 2026.
- **Licence:** the manifest declares `GPL-3.0-or-later`; the [solver licence record](../research/animation/cc-idle.md) documents this. The upstream declaration does not by itself establish licensing of every possible distributed combination, and use in our studies grants no game-asset redistribution rights.
- **Relationship:** documentation/source learning plus execution of the unmodified external numerical solver. GPL source was read without copying code; no solver implementation is in browser code.

**What we learned / used for**

- **Facial solver route:** the documentation and native solver provide a concrete route from animation float tracks through envelopes, limits and corrective poses to facial movement. This changed the idle investigation from treating flat exported bone channels as a dead end to using an existing community solver for mouth, blink and gaze playback. Our adapter loads the pinned checkout without registering Blender UI; it produced 663 frames affecting 253 facial bones, composed with the body idle in the preview. [Bake adapter](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/tools/bake_idle_face.py) and [provenance/limitations](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/idle-face-bake.json) record exact inputs and output. In-game parity, wrinkle rendering and scale-driven effects remain unproven; keep the local game-derived bake out of releases.
- **Wrinkle outputs:** processed wrinkle outputs vary even where raw wrinkle input tracks are constant, and our bake discards them. Re-executing the solver with the Three.js skin adapter distinguished present brow geometry motion from omitted wrinkle shading ([brow idle audit](../research/animation/brow-idle-gap.md)). With WolvenKit animation export, it enabled a 24 September comparison of the separate `ui_closeup_shot_eyes` clip and brow-card trajectories, with no claim that the alternate clip plays in Nathan's live default idle. The 25 September [reproducible wrinkle probe](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/tools/probe_idle_wrinkles.py) executes the same unmodified solver against hash-checked `ui_closeup_shot` intake and maps all 33 processed output indices to source controls and times ([evidence](../research/animation/brow-idle-gap.md#25-september-reproducible-processed-wrinkle-probe)).
- **Eye material precedents:** [eye.py](https://github.com/WolvenKit/Cyberpunk-Blender-add-on/blob/7a4ee793c36d9615946fe87ec9d42cde7568021d/i_scene_cp77_gltf/material_types/eye.py) and [nodes.py](https://github.com/WolvenKit/Cyberpunk-Blender-add-on/blob/7a4ee793c36d9615946fe87ec9d42cde7568021d/i_scene_cp77_gltf/materials/blender/nodes.py) corroborate packed normal reconstruction, DirectX green inversion, roughness-G use and uncertain IOR mapping.
