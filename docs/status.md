## Summary

XF Studio's eye-makeup editor builds a verified **XF Eye Artistry** mod from the player's own game. The first public alpha, **`v0.1.0-alpha.1`**, was tagged but stays an unpublished draft. The first published alpha will be **`v0.1.0-alpha.2`**: it is prepared (version, changelog with known limitations, release preflight passing) and waits only for its tag from `main`.

The export has been **confirmed in game** across three sessions (25–28 September, feminine V, game 2.31):
- The selector (labelled "XF") appears in the character creator, gameplay and photo mode, and switching, clearing and save persistence work.
- The 0.4 mm plate lift holds at extreme close range and while blinking and turning.
- Closed lids are clean on four eye shapes.
- The plate-local texture window's sharper lines show.
- Colour-shifting behaves as designed.

Still open in game:
- How distinct the flat finishes are: inconclusive under the creator's soft light.
- Shimmer: static dots, still experimental.
- The makeup under a head-hiding item: the component is now `hx_`-prefixed so `hide_Head` hides it, a source-derived change not yet seen in game. The 3D preview renders the head, plate, and the V's own skin, face details (makeup, lipstick, cheeks, freckles, blemishes, scars, tattoos, face cyberware), eyes, brows, lashes, hair, piercings and body (arms, hands, nails, feet, body tattoos and scars, under the game's own underwear cover) resolved from the player's installation, with a **Character creator** lighting preset for calibration. The R&D lab has knowledge pages (also published on the site) on materials and shaders, hair shading, creator lighting, the CC file chain, head CC rendering, Glitter in game, mod loading, runtime access and tooling.

## Proven only offline or not at all

Offline verification is not in-game proof. Runtime evidence so far is in the [finish board results](../experiments/016-finish-board/README.md#runtime-results) and the switcher-exclusivity save check ([head CC rendering](../knowledge/head-cc-rendering.md)). Still without runtime evidence, or found wrong in game:

- **Close-up rendering:** settled in game. The 0 mm plate broke up at extreme close range while 0.1 mm and 0.4 mm held (26 September, [experiment 020](../experiments/020-session-2/README.md)). The 0.4 mm plate holds in motion, and closed lids are clean on eye shapes 01, 02, 10 and 12 (28 September, [experiment 028](../experiments/028-session-3/README.md#5-results-28-september-2026)). That closes the eyelid-contact gates of experiments 006/012 at normal viewing; the resolution limit is in the [pipeline guide](../research/authoring/studio-to-mod-pipeline.md#plate-lift-at-build).
- **Finish looks:** Colour-shifting behaves as designed, and the Metal ramp shows no angular highlights. Matte and Satin first read too glossy; since then, offline, Matte's roughness has gone to 1.0 and Colour-shifting's metalness to 0.08. Under the creator's soft light Gloss A–D barely separate, so the finish defaults wait for a controlled-light verdict and ship unchanged in alpha.2. Shimmer shows a static field of dots with no flash: it stays experimental and its facet route is reworked ([finishes backlog](../research/backlog/glitter-material.md#open)).
- **Head-hiding items:** the `hx_` component follows ArchiveXL's `hide_Head` rule from source; no vanilla item carries that tag, so the in-game check needs a mod item ([plan 2.6](../research/runtime/next-sessions-plan.md#n2-finish-close-out-headgear-kill-switch)).
- **Colour parity** of brows, lashes, hair and skin with the game: the creator-lighting preset has shadows and a provisional calibration fitted to two matched captures, the 27 September face page and session 3's hair page: the cyan fills at a third, the key near its data strength, exposure 1. Session 3's hair ladder shows that the preview reproduces the decoded hair bake. In the game, though, light hair is 1.5–1.8 times brighter relative to dark hair and cooler; that lighting gap is open ([hair shading §8](../knowledge/hair-shading.md#creator-ladder-session-3)). The game's screen-space subsurface scattering now runs in the preview ([skin reference §11](../research/materials/shader-skin.md#11-screen-space-scatter-in-the-preview-threejs)). It showed that the lit skin's extra warmth is not scattering (the next suspects are the skin's tone and the light on skin), and that the Studio's nose shadow is lit by the cyan fills where the game's is lit by scatter. Next: the key lights' colour (the silver-piercing frame) for a hue fit, and the ladder with ray-traced lighting off ([creator lighting §12.6](../knowledge/creator-lighting.md#126-refit-from-two-captures-28-september)).
- **Steam and Epic detection** are proven by tests only (GOG on a real machine).
- **Runtime bridge:** works in game (26 September): script calls with a proper context (the earlier TweakDB crash is fixed and checked in game), reads, writes with undo, the kill switch, character-creator presets, photo-mode camera, light, expression and captures. Since then, built and tested offline for the next session: opening photo mode with the player's own key, automatic framing, cursor hiding, light on/off, creator Confirm and Back (test profile only), burst captures, XF camera presets (test builds only), and two read-only expression probes.
- **The masculine V's selector** is verified offline only (a real both-body Build passed the independent verifier; her 16 files are byte-identical to a feminine-only build). Its registration, placement, lids and the shared texture window on his face are [N12](../research/runtime/next-sessions-plan.md#n12-masculine-v-xf-eye-artistry-for-him), which needs a new-game masculine V.
- Glitter export stays guarded; its game route is designed ([experiment 018](../experiments/018-glitter-route/README.md)) but not built.
- **Plate-local UV window:** seen in game. Placement matches (26 September), and the new density is visibly sharper than the head-UV layout at the creator's eye framing (28 September).
