## Geometry result

The exported head has 7,189 vertices and 13,186 indexed triangles, one material and UV0. Counting connected components on the original triangle indices yields **16 UV-split islands**. Their largest component has 4,830 vertices / 9,364 triangles and includes the visible upper lip, lower lip and nearby face skin in the rendered front, quarter and close views. A central 449-vertex / 768-triangle island and two 131-vertex / 192-triangle islands each lie near the mouth in neutral coordinates; the close saved-pose view shows at most a narrow line from a smaller island, while the visible lip surfaces remain in the large island. These component counts and the GLB digest are checked before the page displays island colours. Green denotes the largest island, orange the 449-vertex island, yellow/blue the 131-vertex islands, grey other head islands and cyan the separate eye mesh. Colour names do not assign tissue semantics.

## Conservative exclusion preview

The provisional guard marks neutral head-coordinate y=1.595–1.675 magenta and the remaining head green. It is carried as a vertex-to-fragment coordinate through the **same skinned/morphed geometry**, so the band follows the source head as facial morphs move. The chosen interval deliberately spans much more than the lips; it excludes nose-base and chin-adjacent skin too. It is a visible *do not blur* band, not a source/destination ownership ID or an upper/lower-lip mask. Eye geometry remains a separate cyan mesh; teeth and other mouth parts are absent from this page. Background gaps are dark. No convolution, transport or colour modification is applied.

## Decision and falsifiable next test

**The lip transport gate fails.** Mesh identity and UV connectivity cannot separate the two visible lip surfaces from surrounding skin. The broad exclusion band can avoid altering the visible lips, but then it cannot test lip-region diffuse transport or establish safe exchange near the mouth. This page also lacks source-backed teeth and inner-mouth visibility, an anatomical annotation, a depth/ID-gated source-destination pair test, and a full-radiance/blur/composite pass. A screen blur would still be capable of importing colour across a dark mouth gap or eye edge. The prior [diffuse SSS gate](diffuse-sss-gate-2026-09-24.md) therefore remains closed.

A later off/on trial needs a reviewed annotation tied to this exact triangle/UV geometry that identifies upper lip, lower lip, surrounding skin and mouth interior independently of painted albedo; effective teeth and eye geometry must be present. At front, quarter and mouth-close views in neutral and representative saved/animated poses, render ownership and depth at the intended blur resolution. For every output pixel, reject contributors with a different semantic ID, across a depth discontinuity, or across the silhouette. Inspect the rejected/accepted pairs at both lip corners and all visible tooth/eye edges before changing diffuse pixels. Full and diffuse captures must retain matched skinning, maps, camera and lights; specular must come unchanged from the full capture. If an annotation cannot reliably label the lip folds, exclude the entire mouth region and state that the effect is a non-lip skin study.

Validation: `bun run check`, `bun run build` and the full authoring Bun test suite pass. The diagnostic requires the pinned local head hash and fails visibly if topology counts differ. There was no game launch, private asset commit or production renderer edit.
