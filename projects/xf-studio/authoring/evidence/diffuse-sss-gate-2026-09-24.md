# Diffuse-only SSS study gate — 24 September 2026

The optional subsurface comparison was **not enabled**. The isolated `/render-fidelity-study.html` currently cannot produce a defensible off/on image without first adding surface ownership and a separate diffuse capture. A screen blur of its present canvas would blur specular glints and mix neighbouring lip/eye surfaces; calling that SSS would obscure the white-seam diagnosis. No renderer, shader or user draft was changed for this gate.

## Observed study-page boundary

The [private source-map study](render-fidelity-source-maps-2026-09-24.md) already hash-checks the base D05/Arkhe candidates and matches the base head's static geometry/UV0. But `src/render-fidelity-study.ts` creates **two independent WebGL renderers** and calls `renderer.render(scene, camera)` directly into each visible canvas. There is no full-radiance target, isolated diffuse target, depth texture, head/eye ownership buffer, blur/composite pass or pass disposal/resize path. Its one `head` mesh contains the mouth and surrounding skin as one material; `eyes` are a separate mesh, while teeth and other mouth parts are hidden. `extendSkin` supplies custom eight-influence skinning to the current material shader, so a temporary stock override material cannot be presumed to preserve the same deformed surface. Existing controls move the front camera's height/distance or switch to the saved-eye crop; there is **no three-quarter camera preset**. Thus the requested matched front/three-quarter/mouth-close matrix cannot be captured by the page today.

I inspected the page in Chrome with the base D05/R/packed-normal selection at neutral front and a closer distance. The front still shows the current browser's narrow white lower-lip reflection on the left and a softer but visibly light lip boundary on the D05 right. Moving the current distance slider from 0 to −0.10 sends much of the mouth above the viewport, rather than creating a fixed mouth close-up. The existing broad-crop counter correctly stops reporting comparable metrics after that move. The page loaded its hash-checked source maps without an asset error. No three-quarter or SSS off/on capture was claimed.

The exact first-pass dependency is a **semantic ownership mask**, independent of displayed albedo and provider: skin/eye/teeth, plus a safe boundary around upper lip, mouth gap and lower lip. A depth gate alone can reject separated surfaces and silhouette bleed but cannot reliably separate close folded lip surfaces with similar depth. A colour-key mask derived from either D05 or Arkhe albedo would change with the provider and can fail on makeup, tint or lighting; it is not source authority. The existing native UV0/triangle match makes a future mesh/UV-backed annotation feasible, but no validated lip partition is present. Until that mask is checked at front, three-quarter, close-up and representative morphs, the mouth should be excluded from any screen-space transport trial.

After ownership is validated, an independent browser approximation could capture full radiance and diffuse-only head radiance in linear half-float targets, depth/ownership-gate a short separable blur, and add only the blurred-minus-sharp **diffuse** delta to the original full image. Eyes, teeth, mouth gaps, silhouettes and unvalidated lip edges must have zero contribution; untouched full radiance retains sharp specular. The diffuse capture must use the same eight-influence skinning, morphs, UV, source maps, lights and tone mapping inputs as the full pass. Compare one source set at a time with locked front, three-quarter and mouth-close cameras, then report actual GPU timing and memory. This is an implementation plan, **not** a claim of REDengine skin transport or a recommendation to copy the separate CharacterCreator shader.

For scale only, three full-resolution RGBA16F colour targets (full, diffuse, one blur) plus 32-bit depth have a lower-bound storage of roughly **28 bytes per pane pixel** before ownership, a second blur target, transient copies and renderer overhead. At the previous 782×1230 physical-pixel captures, two panes would use at least **54 MB** for just those attachments; DPR2 relative to that pixel size would quadruple it. No GPU timing was measured because no safe pass was implemented. A lower-resolution diffuse/blur chain may save memory after its quality and boundary errors are measured; it cannot waive the mask gate.

The [read-only CharacterCreator reference](../../../../research/eye-artistry/charactercreator-rendering-reference.md) informed the separation of diffuse transport from sharp specular and the warning about screen/depth limitations. This gate did not copy its code, shader constants or assets. Its masked, depth-guided screen-space pass is an approximation on a different head, not an XF Studio or game acceptance result. Central credit request: retain the CharacterCreator first-party learning entry linked to that note; no new third-party reuse occurred here.

The next bounded prerequisite is an isolated source-backed lip/eye/teeth ownership diagnostic with front/quarter/mouth view presets and a falsifiable no-bleed test. Only if that passes should an off/on diffuse experiment be built. A production renderer change and game launch remain outside this gate.

**25 September follow-up:** the [lip light-component study](render-fidelity-study-2026-09-24.md#light-components-and-mesh-ownership--25-september-2026) now exposes matched standard diffuse and specular views, plus unlit albedo and per-mesh ownership. Its ownership view confirms that the two lips and surrounding skin are one head mesh, while the mouth gap is background and teeth remain hidden. It still has no anatomical lip partition, independent full-radiance target, depth/ownership-gated blur or validated front/quarter/close camera matrix. Thus the earlier SSS off/on gate remains closed; the new views identify the missing boundary more clearly and allow the specular lip line to be inspected without conflating it with diffuse IBL.
