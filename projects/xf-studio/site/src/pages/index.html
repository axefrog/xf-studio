Customisation studio for Cyberpunk 2077

Customise your game, starting with your own V.

XF Studio is a local studio for customising Cyberpunk 2077. It begins with your own V and is intended to grow into other areas of the game. Its first working feature is eye makeup: complete layered looks designed on V’s deforming head and compiled into mod files for one in-game selector.

02Eye makeup: how it works

From a blank layer to one in-game choice

You design complete eye-makeup looks in the Studio and build them into XF Eye Artistry, your own copy of an eye-makeup mod. The game only needs to offer one selector that switches between them, instead of separate menus for every colour, design and finish.

  1. 01 · Author

    Compose a preset

    Stack ordered layers. Draw each with Bézier curves, then set colour, finish, pigment strength and edge softness, working on the 3D head or the flat UV map.

    Works locally
  2. 02 · Keep

    Save to your library

    Collections live in a local SQLite library with immutable revisions, conflict protection and recovery. Portable recipe and collection files cover sharing and backup.

    Works locally
  3. 03 · Check and build

    Make private mod files

    Check lists exactly which layers or presets a package would omit. Build makes your XF Eye Artistry mod files: it compiles a package-only copy, runs an independent verifier and leaves your collection untouched.

    Verified offline
  4. 04 · Choose

    Pick a look in the game

    The goal: one character-creator selector switching between your complete presets, plus Off. Registration, switching and saved selections still need an in-game test.

    Not yet game-tested

03Eye makeup today

What works today

Every eye-makeup capability here is implemented and exercised in the local prototype. It is not a released product, and the browser preview approximates the game’s renderer rather than reproducing it.

  • Presets and layers

    Named presets with ordered layer stacks, up to 32 layers each. Add, copy, rename, reorder and remove both, with per-preset Undo and recoverable removal. Identities survive renames and reordering.

  • Precise shapes

    Bézier paths with smooth, symmetric and corner points, and mirror symmetry. Move, rotate and scale whole shapes, and bend them with up to eight smoothly blended warp fields per layer.

  • Pigment and edges

    Pigment strength can vary from point to point with smooth gradients. Edge softness is set for the whole outline or per point, and stays separate from pigment.

  • Edit on the face or the map

    Drag points, handles, warp fields and whole shapes directly on the moving 3D head, or in a zoomable, pannable UV map. Each gesture is one Undo step, and Escape cancels it.

  • Preview on your V

    V’s head and an expanded eye plate cut from it, skinned and deformed by all 105 facial morphs. Read facial shape choices from your own save, play the character-creator idle, adjust field of view and choose 512 to 4K preview textures. Optional brows, lashes, hair and piercings add context.

    Your files, not ours: the preview reads resources from your own game and mod installation. None are distributed, and materials are browser approximations.

  • Honest mod export

    Check and Build share one filtered snapshot. Layers whose finish has no export adapter, and presets left empty, are named rather than silently packaged. Files are promoted only after an independent verifier passes.

    Every manifest says so: built packages are recorded as not installed and not game-tested.

Seven finish families

Each layer has one finish. All seven preview in the Studio. Matte, Satin and Metallic become mod files through a provisional adapter. Shimmer, Glossy and Colour-shifting have an experimental export built from the game's own decal materials. Nobody has seen any of them in game yet.

04Status

Where the project stands

Reviewed {{statusReviewed}}. The detailed, evidence-linked record is in the repository’s status document.

Established

  • A working local prototype for editable layered eye-makeup presets, the local library and portable files.
  • An offline package pipeline that filters, compiles and independently verifies private mod candidates.
  • A preview head and eye plate matched to the game’s geometry, keeping native skin weights and every facial morph.
  • Matte, Satin and Metallic layers can be packaged through a provisional material adapter.

Still to prove

  • In-game behaviour: selector registration, switching A → B → Off, and saved selections. No package has been installed or tested in the game.
  • Eye-plate clearance under facial animation: the latest candidate corrections failed dense animation checks, so the plate is unchanged.
  • Game materials for Glossy, Shimmer, Colour-shifting and Glitter: the first three build as experiments from the game's own decal materials, but none has been seen in game; Glitter has no game material yet.
  • Preview fidelity: skin, eye and hair shading in the browser remain approximations of the game renderer.

06Directions

Where the studio could go

Eye makeup comes first. Everything beyond it is a direction rather than a commitment: each area is discussed before any building starts, and none has a date.

Next for eye makeup

Active work, in no guaranteed order.

  1. Prove the package in the game

    Selector registration, switching between presets and Off, and saved selections surviving a reload, batched into one prepared test session.

  2. Eye-plate clearance

    A correction that stays clear of the skin through the full facial animation, not just sampled poses.

  3. More exportable finishes

    Game material adapters for the preview-only finishes, but only where the game can represent them faithfully.

  4. A redesigned workspace

    Dockable, floating and tabbed panels, with light and dark themes that follow the system by default.

  5. Library tools

    Visible revision history and restore, backups, thumbnails and search.

07Principles

How the project works

  • Honest status

    Previewed, packaged, verified and game-tested are different claims. The Studio, its package manifests and this site keep them apart.

  • Your work stays local

    Drafts, the library and imported saves stay on your machine. There is no account or cloud service; saves are read, never modified.

  • No game assets shared

    This site hosts no game files, and the repository keeps extracted game and mod assets, saves and personal libraries out of version control. The preview uses resources from your own installation.

  • Credit where it is due

    Techniques learned from other modders and tools are recorded as they inform the work, with the distinction between learning and reuse kept explicit.
