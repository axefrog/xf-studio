# XF Story Engine

Procedural gigs, quests and encounters over a level-of-detail world simulation for Cyberpunk 2077. This
project holds the engine's code. Research and design live in [`research/story-engine/`](../../research/story-engine/README.md).

## What is here

[`reference/`](reference/) is the **reference model**: a TypeScript implementation of the kernel
specification [S0 v3](../../research/story-engine/spec/kernel.md), written as the oracle that a later
native core must agree with. It implements everything in S0 except the items deferred in the
specification's section 0.2 (the memory machinery and implicit edges among them), with the process
additions of S0 v4.14 (a process's own state and digest, `demanded(tier)`, window-keyed draws, backdated
events, silent restatement, nominal kind compatibility), the cores of S1 v0.5 and S2 v1.10, and the procedural core of S3 v0.4.

- The kernel: identity and BLAKE3 hashing, fdlibm `ln`, the schema and function registry, nodes, edges,
  resolution (constraint arbitration, weights, the flat Gumbel-max draw), pins and time, queries, events
  and summaries, evolution (the boundary order), context frames and relevance, beliefs, groups,
  provenance, retention and the logical persistence record.
- [`reference/SPEC-NOTES.md`](reference/SPEC-NOTES.md) lists every place the specification left a choice
  open and what the reference model chose, worst first.
- The tests include the acceptance fixtures F-1 to F-15 and C-a to C-d, the invariants I-1 to I-15,
  regression tests for the findings of the independent reviews (`test/sr1.test.ts`, SR-01 to SR-13, and
  `test/sr2.test.ts`, SR2-01 to SR2-07; the digest test holds SR-14 and SR2-08), and a **determinism digest**: a fixed script of 1,000 mixed operations whose
  every result, and every saved record, folds into one hash (`test/digest.test.ts`). The script covers
  arbitration, time, queries, relevance and dilemmas, processes, beliefs with telling and evidence,
  summaries with canon items, a group's decisions and orders, and saves and replacement loads with
  branches, snapshots and outstanding process batches. A second implementation that agrees on the
  primitive vectors and the digest agrees on the kernel for what the script exercises; what it leaves out
  (read-only loads, fine-tier processes, `explain`, region scopes, the policy hooks beyond the stub) is
  listed in [`reference/SPEC-NOTES.md`](reference/SPEC-NOTES.md#cross-implementation-check) and left to
  the fixtures and unit tests.
- The layer 2 tests live in `reference/test/s2/`: the R1 acceptance tests and three crash-point sweeps, the
  lint matrix (each lint has a tripping seed and the passing base), casting, the lifecycle, the arbiter,
  predicates, pack lifecycle, property tests, and `fixtures.test.ts`, which indexes SF-1 to SF-64 (50 have
  named tests; the rest name the later capability they wait for).

- The **v4 core** of S0 (section 20): derives that get the query time, fact, seed and cast kinds with the offer,
  response and seed-outcome events, `outcome` and `served` on events, the `frame` change, routing to layer 2,
  typed `SettingsSet` content, `age-class`, and staged batches (S0 v4.12). The frozen v3 digest runs unchanged, on the v3 schema with frame changes off, with the
  v4 additions switched off; a second frozen digest covers the run with them on.
- **Layer 2** ([`reference/src/seeding/`](reference/src/seeding/)): the core of S2 v1.10 (section 0.3). Seed
  documents and the text form of predicates, the ladder profile, compilation and the core lints, casting with
  claims, the lifecycle and beats, offers and responses, the arbiter (gates, tempo, session states, prepare and
  confirm through a staged batch, display grades, receipts), the exposure ledger, the fit report, and
  persistence through the kernel's seed records. Later capabilities (clocks, short and full forms, plants,
  language-model fields, city reads, the tone, dread and stage lints and the rest of S2 section 0.3's
  "later" list) are refused at compile time with "not supported yet". The R1 scenario (S2 Appendix A) runs end to end through a save and a load.
  The `city` and `social` atoms (S2 2.16, 2.17) are read through a `SimReadPort` that the simulation layer
  attaches (`reference/src/sim/layer2-wire.ts`): the eleven reads the core builds compile and answer, and
  the five it does not (`heatRank`, `tension`, `reach`, `protected`, `contract`) are refused as "not supported
  yet", as is every read when no simulation layer is attached.
- The **simulation layer** ([`reference/src/sim/`](reference/src/sim/)): the core of [S1 v0.5](../../research/story-engine/spec/simulation.md)
  (section 0.2) over the stand-in slice for Kabuki and the fixture slice FX-3, with every tunable a field of `S1Data`.
  - *Foundations:* the frozen table artifacts (`exptab@1`, `normtab@1`, `lqtab@1`, regenerated by `make-tables.ts`
    to the specified hashes), the arithmetic that uses nothing else, the key registry, the `xf.core.world@1` schema,
    the population fit, and residents regenerated from the household table, with their spells, routines and the
    tie function.
  - *Seven processes:* `city.events` (the one admission owner: immigrants, offspring, regimes, heat and bands, the ring),
    `city.mirror` (the offline replay port's raw events become derived events, with the cover the events process thins by),
    `order.stocks`, `order.norms` (private against public acts, enforcement), `order.ledger` (reputation through carriers),
    `group.decide` (pressures, intentions, retaliation) and the fine tier `scene.acts` (opens the items of the cells in
    recorded focus, publishes each once).
  - *Policies:* `witness`, `social`, `fade`, `ties`, `v.frames` and `reconcile`, behind the kernel's hooks; the registered
    reads with timestamped transitions; the registries for the procedural engine; `Sim.why` and `Sim.remint`.
  - *Audits:* `audit.ts` holds the honesty audits of S1 12 as functions over structure; the tests hand each a fault.
  - The tests (`reference/test/sim/`) hold every unit fixture of S1 14.1 the core covers, the end-to-end fixtures SF1-E1 to
    SF1-E4, the twenty-eight invariants (`invariants.test.ts`) and the property tests of 14.3 (`properties.test.ts`: generated
    scripts, random call groupings and reloads, forgetting, metamorphic relations, limits, demand). Later capabilities (S1 0.3)
    are refused plainly. `S1_FULL=1` runs the long fixtures at the specification's scale.

- The **procedural layer** ([`reference/src/procedural/`](reference/src/procedural/)): the core of
  [S3 v0.4](../../research/story-engine/spec/procedural.md). World-only fitting and its report; senses, pacing,
  bounded holds and joints; derived threads and budgets; the nested schedule; completed-pattern sifting with
  a saved chronological buffer; frozen candidate/kit scores; atomic sowing, quiet pending expiry and a local
  response-evidence log. Pool entries, event descriptors, tombstones, lifetimes, recent history, slot position
  and the arc lock survive replacement loads. Later capabilities receive plain "not supported yet" findings.
  [The fixture index](reference/test/s3/README.md) records coverage and limits, including R1, the literal SF3-37
  script, and Kabuki with S1/S2 attached and fresh-process crashes at each tested window boundary.
  Findings 175–193 in SPEC-NOTES identify contract additions and fixture contradictions for the coordinator.

The model's runtime dependency is `@noble/hashes` (BLAKE3). The source audits use the pinned TypeScript dev dependency's compiler AST API.

## Running the tests

From `projects/xf-story-engine/reference/`:

```
bun install
bun test
bun x tsc --noEmit
```

The review regressions include six A2 checks for same-second tie reopening and atomic regime/price publication, with and without reloads.
All 26 mutation probes must reach their intended semantic assertions;
the guarded full run and timing remain the coordinator's check.
The preceding measured baseline was 1,058 tests in about 83 seconds. Run the full suite under the repository memory guard
with a 4 GB limit, using the full Python interpreter path as the repository rules require. From this directory on Windows:

```powershell
& "$env:LOCALAPPDATA/Python/pythoncore-3.14-64/python.exe" ../../../tools/memory_guard.py --limit 4 -- bun test
```

`S1_FULL=1` restores the specification's long fixtures, the additional uninterrupted 1,008-window replay, and cold checks of all
10,000 tie pairs. Run that profile under the same guard. The regression mutation check is
`bun run test/sim/check-review-mutations.ts` (optionally followed by one probe name, such as `R3-06`): it changes only the targeted
behaviour in memory and requires failure at the named semantic assertion. Setup and import failures are rejected as evidence.

S3 and its kernel regressions cover nominal-query boundaries, the S3-01–07 review fixes, explicit summary-materialisation
ownership and released event-location history through fresh loads. Release preserves actual edge validity; re-reference
replays missing saved records through the same header-first path as load, including swept summary descriptors and
partially recreated item headers, and produces identical later records with or without two loads. The writer records
all changes to earlier saved contributions, edges and pins independently of retention; sweeping protects pending
contribution ends until recorded. The ordinary live-state view stays independent of save frequency.
Reopened intervals keep their own unclamped ends even when their starts match a previous interval, with/without loading
A or sweeping before B. S3G-01 covers all 16 release/end variants, both operation orders and retained-consumer controls.
S3G-02 covers swept descriptor/item recovery in both root orders, incremental/snapshot records and fresh B loads.
S3H-01 covers all 16 re-mint/restore variants with saved terminations; S3H-02 covers four never-saved-header cleanup
variants and recovery against known live headers; S3H-03 covers both general-authored-rule variants. Each has controls.
Sweep and post-save cleanup drop only exact recoverable contributions, independently of the origin's lifetime.
Header re-creation replays saved facts before defaults, and load selects its recovery baseline before reconciliation.
The bounded sweep/replay property test compares retained semantic state after every one of 768 generated operations,
with random saves and fresh loads in both snapshot modes. All 56 source-loader mutation probes reach their intended
semantic assertions; `bun run check` passes. The preceding guarded full-suite baseline was **1,269 tests across 78 files
in 95.44 seconds**, peaking at **1.1 GB** under the 4 GB limit. The expanded run hit the machine-wide floor twice
(0.9 GB and 0.3 GB in the test job); the coordinator confirmed a kernel memory leak, requested no further retries,
and will run the full suite after reboot. Focused S3H, earlier event-persistence, retention and digest tests pass.
Kernel digests stay at v3 `f924d2c9d1159bca85bbfa4cf18dba6c` and v4
`42d5a84e16dde21cc0bca7fe50c01d72`; the S1 frozen transcript is unchanged. The S3 transcript checks independently encoded identities and draws, plus explicit
persisted records. Hand-over facts, pending closure consumption and output-batch event facts survive replacement loads;
NPC/spine filtering has one authority across casting and preparation. The coordinator owns independent review before
integration. No game session is needed. All review fixes remain uncommitted for the coordinator.

## Layout

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `reference/src/` | The kernel, one module per subsystem. `kernel.ts` composes them; `index.ts` exports the public surface. |
| `reference/src/seeding/` | Layer 2: seed documents, predicates, compile and lint, casting, lifecycle, arbiter, effects, persistence. `layer2.ts` composes them. |
| `reference/src/sim/` | The simulation layer: tables and arithmetic, data, index slices, schema pack, population, ties, the processes (`proc-*.ts`, `ledger.ts`), the policies, reads, audits. `sim.ts` composes them; `layer2-wire.ts` attaches layer 2. |
| `reference/src/procedural/` | Layer 3: typed data, keys, copied views, fit, director, pattern/kit lint, sifter, sower, persistence and audits. `layer3.ts` composes them. |
| `reference/test/`, `reference/test/s2/`, `reference/test/sim/` | One test file per subject; `fixtures.test.ts` indexes the acceptance fixtures; `synthetic.ts` is the schema and operation script behind the invariant and digest tests; `test/sim/` holds the S1 fixtures (`helpers.ts`, `feed.ts`, `gen.ts` and `records.ts` are shared tooling, not tests). |
| `reference/SPEC-NOTES.md` | Interpretations of the specification, for its owner to confirm. |
