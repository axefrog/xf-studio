# Scoring sheet

Built from the quality bar of dossier 23 (5.2) and the criteria sheet (5.5). One item per quality, rated separately and never added.

| Item | Asked as | Bar |
|---|---|---|
| caused | Does it read as something that happened for a reason (rather than being arranged to give you a task)? 1 = no reason at all, 7 = clearly caused. | Causality (bar 1; C1) |
| legible | Is it clear what is going on and what a person in your position could do about it? 1 = baffling, 7 = completely clear. | Legibility (bar 3; C4) |
| setting | Does it sound like it belongs in a Night City district: its people, its pressures, its voice? 1 = could be anywhere, 7 = unmistakably here. | Setting fidelity (bar 6; C9) |
| pull | Would you want to follow this up? 1 = not at all, 7 = at once. | Hook strength (bar 2 and 4; C2, C11) |
| made_by | person or program, and the cue | Feels generated (C8; bar 5 and 6) |
| pairs | which of two would you rather follow up; which reads as more caused | the decision rule of P-D |

Cards: 96 (24 per arm over 4 arms). Pairs: 96. Raters: 4, each with a different balanced order (the second is the first reversed).

Decision rule: the engine beats the baseline when, over raters, its share of forced choices against A3 is above one half beyond the sign test (two-sided p < 0.05) on both questions, and its mean on "caused" and "pull" is above A3's. Selection (E against A1) is credited only if E beats A1 by the same rule; otherwise the simpler design stands (PNL-11). Detection (made_by) is reported by arm.
