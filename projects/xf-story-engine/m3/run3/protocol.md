## Packet schema 3: identical canonical accounts

The original seeded sample and shared configuration quotas remain unchanged: 96 sampled memberships, 24 per arm and three per arm/configuration. Group memberships globally only when their ordered occurrence IDs, full account text in both renderers, response semantics (tokens, stance, requested verb, protagonist/completion flags and response links), and L2 tokens all agree. Equal IDs alone are insufficient. This stricter rule splits four former groups (H-021, H-022, H-035 and H-047); [review-fix evidence](review-fixes.json) lists each membership split. The packet shows 79 canonical accounts once each. A deterministic representative supplies the displayed account; the hidden key retains every sampled source's arm, configuration, thread, run, start and occurrence times, plus per-arm multiplicity. Eleven groups share identical accounts; eight span configurations. Unique per-arm accounts are E 19, A1 20, A3 24 and A5 24. Both schema-2 rating passes are superseded; both fresh schema-3 passes have now completed on 79 accounts and 42 pairs each. This canonicalisation affects the blind packet, not the underlying engine-thread population measures.

Assign one renderer to each identical canonical account, balancing the original multiplicity-weighted E/A1 halves globally and within configurations. All memberships of that account use the same renderer; the second sheet reverses it. A5 renderer and length remain independently balanced, six accounts per cell. All twelve matched prefixes per sheet exactly meet the original E targets. A3 keeps its template rendering. This constrained balancing uses memberships only, never scores.

Per-arm, per-configuration and crossed-cell scores weight the single canonical answer by its original arm multiplicity. Also report unweighted means and seen-before counts, counting each canonical account once per arm (once per arm/configuration in stratified results). A shared score is correlated across its memberships; no pooled significance test is performed.

Pairs remain within configuration and renderer. Omit self and identical-text pairs, retain their reasons and count them as ties alongside E/A1 wins and losses. The completed packet shows 42 pairs per sheet: 18 E/A1, twelve E/A5-full and twelve E/A5-matched; six E/A1 self pairs are omitted ties. Sides remain balanced in each shown comparison cell and mirrored for the other sheet.

Generate the original counterbalanced sampled-account order, map it to canonical IDs, then retain only each identical account's first appearance. Preserve every account's internal chronology. Across-configuration shared accounts create cyclic relative-order constraints, so all runs' across-account chronology cannot be preserved simultaneously; the coordinator approved first-appearance ordering and retention of all original times in hidden memberships.

Report exact occurrence-ID sequence E/A1 and E/A5 overlap per configuration for the full populations and the sample, including the denominator and same-arm multiplicities. Also list all shared groups and which configurations share their sequence. This occurrence-ID overlap is distinct from account equivalence: it does not guarantee equal rendered evidence, response semantics or L2 tokens, and does not claim identical hidden state. A5's separate synthetic occurrence identities make zero E/A5 exact-identity overlap structurally expected, so it cannot be interpreted as story novelty. R1's role-masked recast comparison remains separate.

The coordinator also authorised S2 open casting for a nominal target with no discoverable structural members anywhere (including saved headers). This global test precedes scope/predicate filtering. Candidate queries cover compatible kinds in kind-id order; their union is deduplicated in first-occurrence order (kind id, then query result order) before all existing checks and the keyed draw. `Kernel.query` remains structural. Direct person/group, unchanged structural casts, reload ordering and both frozen digests are regression requirements. Person-only harness roles query `xf:resident`, the structural S1 population, rather than the nominal `core:person` classification. This retains the person constraint without widening it to groups; a regression casts unbound person and actor roles against the actual S1 population. Method marker `reviewed-v5` invalidates all preliminary cells, including both old calm cells; every production cell regenerates with these rules.
