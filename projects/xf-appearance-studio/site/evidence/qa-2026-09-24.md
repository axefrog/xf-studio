## Visual review

Full-page and section captures were inspected at normal size, with the finish row also inspected at 2×. Defects found by QA or visual review and fixed before commit:

- **Mobile hero overflow (598 px at 390 px width).** The spec strip's `auto-fit` grid inflated the implicit hero column. Fixed with a `minmax(0, 1fr)` column, a fixed 2×2 strip and `figure { margin: 0 }`.
- **Light-theme warning tags at 4.38:1.** The warning ink was darkened to `oklch(.48 .12 60)`.
- **Oversized “Discussion first” tags.** The class `.future` collided with the card class, so the tag inherited card styles. It is now `.tag.later`.
- **Layout faults.**
  - The fourth spec-strip item wrapped over a grey filler.
  - Finish tiles broke 5 + 2; they now run 7 / 4 / 2 across by width, with bottom-aligned tags.
  - The docs-section eyebrow was oversized.
  - The mobile nav's top border stopped short.
  - The release-status tag stretched full width.
- **Mechanical dot grids in shimmer/glitter swatches.** Replaced with offset, coprime tile sizes. At 2× a faint regular grain is still visible, and each swatch remains labelled as a CSS illustration.
- **Credits wording.**
  - “the author’s own installed copy” was ambiguous and now reads “a Studio user’s”.
  - Alliekat’s gradient was corrected from hair to the saved **brow** colour, per the record.

Observed and accepted:

- The illustration uses the neutral stage in both themes, as the style guide requires.
- The forced-colours capture keeps borders, links and the current nav item legible.
- The 404 page served from a nested path loads its assets.

## Limits

- Chromium only. Safari and Firefox rendering, real mobile devices and screen-reader output were not tested. `corner-shape: bevel` renders only in Chromium; other browsers show the documented square 2 px corners.
- The GitHub Actions workflow was not run, because nothing was pushed and Pages is not enabled. Its YAML parsed, and the pinned actions' `action.yml` inputs and outputs were read. `actionlint` was unavailable.
- The contrast check samples text against composited background colours. Non-text UI contrast relies on the Studio style guide's token audit.
