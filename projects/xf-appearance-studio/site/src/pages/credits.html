Credits

Built on the modding community’s work

XF Studio builds on the discoveries, tools and creativity of the Cyberpunk 2077 modding community. This page summarises who taught us what. The complete record, with versions, commits and exact uses, is the community credits and learning record in the repository.

Acknowledgement is not endorsement, and credit is not permission. Listing a project here does not mean its authors endorse XF Studio. Neither this site nor the repository distributes any of the mod assets named below; where a mod’s files inform the private local preview, they come from the user’s own installation.

01Frameworks, tools and documentation

The foundations

  • ArchiveXL

    By psiberx and contributors

    Its character-creator, mesh and material extension source showed exactly how options register, templates clone and dynamic material paths expand. It is the technical foundation for replacing the old per-combination material matrix with one selector.

    Source learning · build targetRepository

  • Cyberpunk 2077 Modding Wiki

    manavortex and the contributor community

    Special thanks to manavortex for writing much of the wiki and helping keep it available to modders. Its resource, material, CCXL and decal guides, and their screenshots, framed much of our resource-chain research.

    Documentation learningWiki

  • WolvenKit

    The WolvenKit team and contributors

    Save-format facts, shader-cache structures and mesh, morph and animation conversion. Its command-line tools make extraction, serialization and round-trip verification possible.

    Tool use · format learningRepository

  • Cyberpunk Blender Add-on / IO Suite

    Its authors and the RED Modding maintainers

    Its documentation and native facial solver opened the route from animation tracks to real facial movement, and its material code informed the eye preview.

    Source learningRepository

  • Mod Organizer 2 Cyberpunk support

    6788, Zash and contributors

    Its plugin and guide separate MO2’s virtual file priority from archive load order, which corrected the design of our mod-source resolver.

    Source and documentation learningRepository

  • Three.js

    mrdoob and contributors

    The browser renderer behind the preview: skinning, raycasting, cameras and the material hooks that the finish studies extend.

    DependencyRepository

  • Blender

    The Blender Foundation and contributors

    The eye-plate pipeline, geometry diagnostics and contact-visibility studies.

    Tool useblender.org

  • Bun and SQLite

    Bun and SQLite contributors

    The local server and test runner, and the transactional library that keeps immutable preset revisions.

    DependenciesBunSQLite

  • NumPy, SciPy and LZ4

    NumPy, SciPy and LZ4 developers

    Numerical eye-plate feasibility studies, and the block-format specification behind the read-only save reader’s decompression.

    Research dependencies · specificationNumPySciPyLZ4 spec

02Mods

Mods that taught us concrete techniques

Authorship as recorded in the learning record from each mod’s page. “Private local preview” means a Studio user’s own installed copy is read by their local preview; nothing is redistributed.

  • Hair Color Profiles CCXL

    island_dancer and psiberx

    Shared material templates, dynamic material paths and explicit character-creator colour definitions, and the lesson that colour-profile patches can reach brow and lash meshes as well as hair.

    Learning from resource inspectionNexus Mods

  • Photoreal Eyes CCXL

    island_dancer and psiberx

    Thirty-two appearance stubs sharing one material template: concrete evidence that material duplication can shrink dramatically.

    Learning from resource inspectionNexus Mods

  • Heterochromia Eyes - CCXL

    icxrus

    Separate left and right appearance routing: a real example of independently selected components.

    Learning from resource inspectionNexus Mods

  • Soft Natural Eyelashes - CCXL

    icxrus

    Lash geometry and deformation for the preview, and the lesson that mod resources must be resolved through the mod manager, not the game folder alone.

    Learning · private local previewNexus Mods

  • Beautiful EYEBROWS II - CCXL

    Arkhe

    Copy and patch declarations that complete resources from vanilla geometry, and a two-map alpha brow material; its style 18 gives nearby brow context in the preview.

    Learning · private local previewNexus Mods

  • Unique Eyes to CCXL

    nutboy, whose credits acknowledge psiberx, icxrus, island dancer and halvkyrie

    Resolved a saved eye choice through a shared appearance, material patch and texture fallback chain.

    Learning from resource inspectionNexus Mods

  • Kala’s Eyes Standalone V2

    Kala; source eye texture credited to Sarah Cartwright

    Supplies the saved eye maps shown in the private preview, and explained why a generic study texture looked different.

    Private local previewNexus Mods

  • PRC jewellery framework and packs

    eagul, whose framework credits Auska and manavortex

    A morph-compatible, slot-based route for modular jewellery, informing attachment and deformation research for possible future piercing work.

    Learning · private local previewFrameworkMirrors

  • Natural Hair Tones

    Alliekat

    The hair-tone gradient behind the saved brow colour, used for brow context in the preview.

    Private local previewNexus Mods

  • MELUMINARY Long Length Pak Vol. 3 #011

    Creator attribution not yet confirmed

    Its registration and skinned meshes resolve a saved hairstyle for the optional hair preview, and showed why each preview binding needs its own fidelity report.

    Private local previewNexus Mods

03Research and references

Ideas from further afield

  • Discrete Stochastic Microfacet Models

    Jakob, Hašan, Yan, Lawrence, Ramamoorthi and Marschner, 2014

    Why tiny normal-mapped glitter aliases and flickers, framing the glitter preview studies.

    Conceptual learningPaper

  • Real-time Image-based Lighting of Glints

    Kneiphof and Klein, 2025

    Identified environment lighting as a separate filtering requirement for glints.

    Conceptual learningPaper

  • Glint rendering on anisotropic grids

    Thomas Deliot and Laurent Belcour, 2023

    A pixel-footprint counting lead for glitter density.

    Conceptual learningPaper

  • Glitter facet feedback

    island_dancer

    Feedback on facet tilt that shaped the direct-light glitter preview targets.

    Visual feedback

  • Cyberpunk 2077

    CD PROJEKT RED

    The original game art, rigs, facial setups, animation and materials underpin the local preview and experiments. The studio’s “A World Full of Substance” technical-art article informed the case for shared surfaces and masks.

    Game-derived references, kept local

  • Eye Artistry

    axefrog

    The original layered eye-makeup concept, generator and hand-painted art set XF Studio’s starting requirements, and the expanded eye plate is its authored starting geometry.

    Predecessor project
