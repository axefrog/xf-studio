Pan with right-drag to put an eye near the viewport centre before a close lens change; scroll then zooms further. Front view at 10° fits even a narrow editor pane. If the camera range prevents full scale preservation, the lens control shows a note and Front view restores a useful frame. The 0.1 close limit is unchanged; [measurements and remaining limits](../../../research/authoring/camera-zoom-design.md#implementation-checkpoint--24-september-2026).

## Use

**Faster makeup updates:** the optimized raster preserves texture output while skipping irrelevant field calculations and reusing exact mirrored pixels. A supplied complex 2K design now updates in about one second in Chrome, previously about seven seconds. Reload an existing tab to load the new worker. Higher resolution and more complex fields still cost more; [measurements](../../../research/authoring/raster-performance.md).

**Bezier handles over eyes:** tangent arms can now extend and be dragged across eyeballs and plate openings. They are direction guides attached to their parent point, rather than points that must sit on painted geometry. They remain visible only while their parent is visible and facing you. The actual makeup outline points still follow the head surface. Undo and Escape preserve the usual gesture behaviour.

**Preview quality:** expand Preview quality beside Head & lighting to choose 512, 1K, 2K or 4K generated makeup textures. The choice and panel state survive reload. 512/1K suit responsive editing; higher resolutions help inspect fine edges but complex shapes take longer to bake. The last completed preview remains visible while replacements calculate. Rebuild preview retries failed work. Capacity messages explain when to lower quality or disable layers. Imported head/eye assets retain their source detail. Recipes, Undo and export are independent; the current mask export remains 2048 square. [Implementation and limits](../../../research/authoring/preview-quality-contract.md).

**Move the whole shape:** drag the active makeup's painted interior in UV or on the head. Existing handles take priority. Hold Shift while dragging to rotate around the selected point; Shift-scroll scales around it, including warp reach and falloff widths. A move stops at the layer's limits without distorting individual points. Each drag or short wheel burst has one Undo entry; Escape cancels an active gesture. On-head gestures require Surface controls. In UV, ordinary scroll zooms around the pointer and right-drag pans; the crop persists independently of the recipe. [Gesture contract and action API](../../../research/authoring/shape-gesture-contract.md).

**Warp controls:** add up to eight local pulls per layer, select one using its Warp button or a handle, and remove any or all of them. The circle moves the origin; the square sets direction/magnitude. Selected warp reach controls the smooth Gaussian influence; the ring marks that reach, not a hard cutoff. Pulls add smoothly where they overlap, and a new zero-strength control leaves the design unchanged. Both UV and face handles edit the same fields with whole-gesture Undo/Escape. Selection survives layer/preset switches and reload. Strong overlapping pulls can fold/distort the mask; this is not a rigid shape cage.

New portable recipes begin as `xfs/recipe-7`. The Glitter **Preview model** selector offers Classic reflective flakes, Irregular raster flakes, Direct-light glints, Clustered fine glints and Dense fine speckles. Each selection is undoable and exposes only the selected model's settings. The local workspace remembers each model's settings for that layer and preset while you compare them, including across reloads; the portable recipe and SQLite revision store only the selected model. Opening that recipe elsewhere starts other models at their defaults. Direct-light, clustered and dense selections upgrade the recipe schema to `xfs/recipe-8`, `xfs/recipe-9` and `xfs/recipe-10` respectively. Older recipes retain their original optical model and masks. Stored historical SQLite revisions remain unchanged. Preview, PNG export and the flat preset compiler share the same mask evaluator.

**Irregular raster flakes:** Field density, size, size variation, orientation spread and flake colour are editable independently of the layer's base pigment colour. Density is a percentage of generated candidate IDs, **not a visible flake count**. The readout measures approximate centres in the painted shape and atlas pixels with flake coverage after the worker finishes. The default concentrates fine irregular facets in two fixed eye UV regions; authored pigment outside those regions is rejected for the dense setting instead of receiving a misleading partial flake field. Sparse settings retain full-atlas coverage. The candidate is an experimental browser preview with 16 subpixel samples and ordinary mip filtering. **Its visual match to the supplied fine-glitter photos is still blocked:** at face distance most fine facets average into weak grain instead of bright clustered glints over continuous sheen. The UV pane remains a shape coverage editor, and game preset compilation still rejects Glitter. Changing the layer to another finish converts its flake settings to legacy values in one undoable edit. Recipe-7 stores the complete candidate settings through local drafts, collections and SQLite. The combined worker publishes mask, optical maps, sRGB albedo and measurements as one complete appearance; colour edits reuse cached field coverage. A conservative generated-texture estimate counts the fourth map, scratch and bounded cache. A 4K candidate is rejected under the current 1 GiB budget; use 2K or below. [Integration evidence and limits](../../../research/materials/glitter-browser-checkpoint.md).

**Direct-light glints:** this model evaluates deterministic UV-anchored fine polygons against the preview lights, with adjustable density, fine-facet share, strength and facet colour over the layer's base colour. It stores `uv-cell-direct-1` in recipe-8; you can switch back to the raster study without changing old recipe-7 files. This model uses the cancellable worker for the complete shape mask, then draws glints directly in the browser shader, so 1K and 2K look similar at the same camera. In the isolated close-eye comparison its bright screen features were substantially smaller and more numerous than the raster candidate's, while the base kept a continuous sheen. The reference photographs still show denser clusters and less graphic streaks. The game compiler still rejects Glitter. [Direct-light evidence and remaining limits](../../../research/materials/direct-glint-browser-checkpoint.md).

**Clustered fine glints:** this explicitly versioned browser option (`uv-cell-direct-2`, recipe-9) groups deterministic fine facets into soft patches, reduces oversized white streaks and keeps a few specks visible farther out. The local density control is an upper envelope, not a measured visible count. Photo references still have much denser sparkle across the central lid, and this remains an experiment with no game export. [Visual comparison and measured limits](../../../research/materials/clustered-direct-glint-study.md).

**Dense fine speckles:** the recipe-10 model (`uv-cell-direct-3`) lays a much denser population of tiny tilted polygons over a sparse population of larger UV-anchored facets that survives more of the face-scale minification. It has its own default strength, which you can adjust independently. The independent facet colour can match the base pigment more closely. At one fixed comparison crop, the new model had roughly three times the recipe-9 bright screen area close to the eye and retained more face-scale points; it may still look too frosty on some shapes and lighting. Candidate counts in its diagnostic tool are **not** visible sparkles. This remains browser-only and Glitter game export stays blocked. [Counts, visual proxy and performance limits](../../../research/materials/fine-speckle-browser-study.md).

**Curve handles:** new layers use Bézier curves. Select a point to expose its gold tangent handles in UV and on the head. Smooth keeps the two arms aligned, Symmetric also matches their lengths, and Corner lets each arm move independently. Double-click near a curve section to insert a point on it without changing the continuous shape. Older shapes keep automatic curves until you choose Enable Bézier handles; Undo restores the original. Conversion preserves the analytic curve but finer sampling can slightly change edge pixels. Handles outside the plate remain editable in UV, and Fit shape includes them. Very long/tangled handles can currently make preview generation slow. [Verification and measured limits](evidence/bezier-controls-2026-09-23.json).

Drag either sidebar divider to resize it; widths survive reload. Focus a divider and use arrow keys (Shift for larger steps), Home/End for limits, or double-click to reset. Escape cancels a drag. Smaller windows constrain widths to retain preview space without discarding preferred widths; below 900px the panels stack vertically. Scrollbars use the studio colours.

Run `bun install --frozen-lockfile`, then `bun start` from this directory. The server binds only to `127.0.0.1:4317`; set `PORT` if needed. It serves local files and a validated local collection-library API; it cannot modify save games. Startup rebuilds the browser bundle; after editing source, run `bun run build` and reload the page.

## Implementation boundaries

- `src/recipe.ts`: versioned data, validation, deterministic curve/field/coverage evaluator. Mask output is independent of palette.
- `src/uv-view.ts` and `src/path-edit.ts`: pure UV framing/reflection and nearest-section insertion. `src/uv-editor.ts` owns canvas presentation/gestures through application hooks; it does not own recipe evaluation or persistence. [65-test and browser checkpoint](evidence/uv-editor-2026-09-23.json).
- `src/field-ui.ts` adapts field add/remove/selection/reach controls; `field-selection.ts` validates editor-only selections by stable IDs. Recipe evaluation owns smooth additive displacement, independent of UI.
- `src/raster-worker.ts`: off-main-thread mask generation, also used for PNG baking.
- `src/scene.ts`, `src/skin.ts`: actual head/plate rendering, full influence skinning, morphs and exploratory pose.
- `src/idle-animation.ts`: composition of decoded body and offline-solved facial motion, independent loop clocks, pause/seek, separate contributions, world-bind transfer and exact reset. `src/motion-ui.ts` adapts playback controls without owning animation logic. Scene framing always uses the phase-zero head displacement, including after restoring a nonzero paused phase.
- `src/surface-map.ts`, `src/surface-editor.ts`: triangle anchors, continuous UV coverage guard, deformed face handles, visibility checks and pointer gestures. Head/eyes occlude picking; transparent brow/lash textures are not used as opaque blockers.
- `src/save-reader.ts`: bounded, read-only CSAV/LZ4/appearance parser; no save writer. See [save import research](../../../research/eye-artistry/save-import.md).
- `src/workspace-state.ts`: versioned workspace validation, legacy-draft migration, camera/library/preview state and storage isolation. UI and renderer adapters apply its values.
- `src/collection-workspace.ts`, `src/collection-session.ts`: pure collection operations, validation, draft switching, per-preset history and recoverable removal/opening.
- `src/collection-store.ts`: SQLite migration, immutable atomic snapshots, per-preset versions and conflict validation.
- `src/collection-ui.ts`, `src/reorder-ui.ts`: accordion/network adapter and reusable pointer drag feedback.
- `src/package-action.ts`, `src/package-server.ts`: typed collection package request and same-origin localhost boundary around the existing verified CLI.
- `src/main.ts`: editing, workspace autosave/restore, recipe import/export and UI.
- `tools/`: reproducible Blender intake and command-line save inspection.

This is project-local exploratory work that can later inform character rendering. It does not establish another general modding toolbox.

## Library storage

Ignored developer data: `data/library.sqlite`; isolated UI checks use `data/verification.sqlite`. Override the directory with `XFAS_DATA_DIR`. For a consistent live backup, run `bun tools/backup-library.ts`; it creates timestamped copies under `data/backups` using SQLite VACUUM INTO and verifies integrity. Both local databases were backed up before the v2 migration. Do not copy only a running WAL database file. Production packaging will use a user-data location. Schema v2 adds immutable collection revisions and per-preset versions. The transactional v1 migration gathers latest looks into a collection and retains every legacy look revision unchanged. Recipe/name changes advance preset versions; order-only changes advance the collection revision. Removal does not delete history, and restoring old content never reuses an earlier preset revision. Future-version databases are rejected. Revision browsing, automatic backup scheduling and DB autosave remain queued. Imported V and game assets are not stored in this recipe library.

## Continuous point pigment

New layers blend point strengths smoothly across the shape. **Selected point pigment** sets a point's colour-strength target; **Point blend** softens differences between nearby targets. It is separate from **Edge softness**, which controls the outline. A zero-strength point can retain some pigment from nearby points. Increasing blend can help with very narrow opposing transitions; it does not change the overall shape or simulate directional feathering.

Existing saved looks preserve their original blending. Enable **Smooth point gradients** on each layer you want to upgrade; Undo restores the earlier look. The switch and blend distance travel with recipe/collection exports and SQLite saves. New and reset layers default to smooth; duplicated layers retain their source mode.

Portable recipes now use `xfs/recipe-4`. V1–v3 migrate to explicit `legacy-nearest` strength without changing legacy mask pixels; their old SQLite revision bytes stay unchanged. Current layers carry either that mode or `smooth-boundary` with a positive control-UV blend distance, default 0.0005 and allowed range 0.000125–0.02. Positive regularization gives self-crossing paths a defined blend. The geometry and existing ten-step Catmull–Rom approximation are unchanged. This release does not introduce Bézier handles or directional softness.

`pigment-strength.ts` prepares an arclength blend once per raster. `pigment-edit.ts` supplies typed, UI-independent commands; `pigment-ui.ts` adapts the controls to existing Undo and persistence hooks. Worker preview, PNG masks and the preset compiler use the same raster evaluator. [Evidence](evidence/continuous-pigment-2026-09-23.json) records numerical, compatibility, persistence and browser checks. Finite-resolution sampling, more complex layer responsiveness and actual game rendering remain separate concerns.

Reproduce offline comparisons with `bun tools/validate-pigment.ts` (Python/Pillow for the labelled PNG). Generated results stay in `data/pigment-validation/`. Three fixtures at 1K/2K remove the old internal strength seams; all 491,520 compiler alpha checks match the expected squared-alpha encoding. One-layer warm median CPU costs were about 49/186 ms for six knots and 182/846 ms for 24 knots at 1K/2K. Thin-edge downsample differences can still reach 27 alpha levels: continuity does not guarantee sufficient texture sampling.

### Mask responsiveness

Preview calculations now yield cooperatively and cancel obsolete snapshots. Only complete masks for the latest recipe are displayed; preset switching invalidates old versions, and active-layer work can preempt another layer without dropping it. Worker failures preserve queued newer edits and retry lazily, avoiding a restart loop if the script cannot load. Synchronous raster/compiler output is unchanged. The displayed mask time includes cooperative waiting. Preparation and each small pixel batch remain synchronous; this improves responsiveness, not the inherent cost of extreme curves.

### Per-point softness and sharp UV guides

Enable **Per-point edge softness**, select a point in UV or on the head, then adjust **Selected point softness**. Disable it to return to one global width; custom widths remain stored. Widths blend smoothly, so close opposing edges can influence one another. Older designs migrate unchanged to recipe-6.
