XF Holotable · phase 1 · layout mock-up

One model, every view

Everything below is drawn from one small data model: seeded terrain and buildings, items on a timeline, layers and their projection rules. Toggle a layer, scrub the timeline, pick an actor or hold to show story content, and every view changes together. Places and people are made up.

In game: the overlay

A translucent plate the bridge draws itself. It takes no input, so the game plays on underneath. H shows or hides it and M switches between radar only and radar with markers (try them here). The radar turns with V; the markers sit on the people and places they describe. The overlay is always live: scrubbing below never moves it into the past.
