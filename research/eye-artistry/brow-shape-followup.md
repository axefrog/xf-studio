# Saved brow 18 shape follow-up

24 September 2026. Read-only resource audit in the isolated `codex/brow-shape-audit` worktree. No game launch, install, authored geometry, saved recipe or browser material changed. Game and mod binaries, serialized JSON and decoded images remain ignored local inputs. This follows the [saved material correction](brow-lash-fidelity.md) and the supplied comparison showing a thicker-looking studio brow.

## What the current sources establish

The captured save's app hash `10685882159528859062` and definition `10_brown_ombre` refer to Arkhe Beautiful EYEBROWS II FULLER style 18. A fresh unbundle of that installed archive's female `.inkcharcustomization` (SHA-256 `47576629b20b24c597ac63c6d3952bdbff57791dbb93e77086be7ca01b8eb9ee`) shows its `ark_eyebrows_02_ccxl_18` option pointing directly at `ark_eyebrows_02_ccxl_18_pwa.app`; that option includes `10_brown_ombre` at definition index 18. The app's compiled component names the style-18 morph resource (`5838896660247859963`). Its `meshAppearance` is the base `black_carbon`; the mesh exposes `brown_ombre` among its appearance names and its local `@brows` material contains the dynamic gradient path. This checks the saved style/colour registration without treating the base compiled appearance name as the saved colour.

The `.xl` copies the vanilla female brow morph and mesh to `arkhe_copy`, then patches `blob`, `boundingBox` and `targets` into the style morph stub, and `renderResourceBlob` into the style mesh stub. The 2.31 source mesh is resource hash `93420395318360365` in `basegame_4_appearance.archive` (SHA-256 `a51d4940e56cca582e5f026a4a8359a9c2f848fb204f64259582febfd1f9ca15`). The browser's ignored `brows.glb` still hashes to `da1e38700d2549335d6c6b9bf8ad12899fc8dbb2d9b25c8ce70de77db80edcae`, matching the prior vanilla brow morph export. The earlier geometry audit reports 390 vertices; the asset test verifies 105 morph targets and the second skin-weight set. Thus there is no source evidence that style 18 supplies a narrower authored vertex silhouette that the preview should substitute.

The partial style-18 mesh has zero serialized `boneNames` and `boneRigMatrices`; the native vanilla mesh has 74 of each. The patch declaration lists only `renderResourceBlob` for meshes. The selected style's morph blob is null before the patch. This is a **limit on the offline comparison**, not proof of a runtime fault: the rendered buffer and runtime patch/binding may carry the needed deformation data, and the photographed game session did not provide an assembled-resource dump. A vanilla `.glb` can reproduce the source vertex data and morph targets but cannot establish that all engine binding and shading behavior matches.

A captured ArchiveXL 1.26.3 log from **16 September 2026** provides a historical assembly check: `captures/20260923-085239-949631-foundation-existing-logs/mo2-overwrite/archivexl/ArchiveXL-2026-09-16-11-14-16.log` records loading `Arkhe_Beautiful_Eyebrows_02_FULLER_CCXL.xl` (line 31), replacing style 18's `brown_ombre` base-colour appearance (line 8959), and expanding `brown_ombre` from `black_carbon` and instantiating `brown_ombre@brows` (lines 9901/9904). This reduces concern that the partial `.xl` could never assemble, but does not establish which resources won in AutoSave-12's **13 September** session or prove rendered pixels. A separate Ratstick patch also adds `10_washed_brown_ombre`; the captured save selects `10_brown_ombre` and those definitions must not be conflated.

The [installed provider audit](brow-texture-audit.md) found the FULLER archive as the sole installed provider of the selected Arkhe hashes among 1,103 indexed MO2/manual mod archives. Rehashing its current container gave the same SHA-256 `62bc69d17a7f1f0c28e8f12e219e1c717a44d2f3386c40ffdee0c222b99456c0`. The current private browser manifest binds that saved app/definition to the audited primary and secondary PNG digests and Alliekat `brown_ombre` gradient; the primary image still hashes to `5fac5306ee4f32c082a6739457170e5f5d2aac5e2f3a73eb3c903628ebfdb56f`. These are installed and local-preview candidates, **not proven effective winners** in the earlier reference game capture.

## Why a further shape correction is not justified yet

The pre-correction side-by-side image shows a paler, softer-edged studio brow than the photographed game brow. The [material study](brow-lash-fidelity.md) subsequently replaced the diffuse-green fallback with both source alpha channels and the installed gradient; the controlled browser comparison moved the brow darker and slimmer. The photographs use different camera, expression, lighting and makeup, and do not show the corrected browser result under a matched game pose. Their apparent widths are not calibrated geometric measurements.

The browser still draws a separately lit, transparent Three.js `MeshStandardMaterial` over its approximate head shader. The installed `mesh_decal_double_diffuse.mt` is a post-G-buffer decal with a packed normal input (`NormalAlpha≈0.8`, `UseNormalAlphaTex=1`); the preview does not reproduce that normal/skin shading path. This could change perceived density and contour, but its size and direction in a matched capture remain unmeasured. The game's diffuse and secondary XBMs specify 2048×1024 true-colour mip chains (`TEXG_Generic_Color`, primary `isGamma=1`, secondary `isGamma=0`), while the browser decodes their base PNGs and generates its own mips. A mip-filtering difference is a testable hypothesis, not a demonstrated cause. The current source-backed response is to preserve the selected style, geometry and corrected alpha maps.

## Next evidence gate

The [authored-mip follow-up](brow-lash-mip-gate.md) now finds the source alpha mip chain within one byte per pixel of a simple box reduction through the useful levels; it found no large authored density change to justify thinning the preview. Before a game session, audit whether the partial mesh's empty bone metadata changes an assembled export. For the planned batched game capture, record the actual effective archive/XL winners and compare a saved V with the corrected studio result at matched head pose, camera angle, field of view and light. Judge brow silhouette and coverage separately from colour and skin shading. Do not alter style or thin the mesh from the unmatched photographs.
