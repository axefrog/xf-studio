# CharacterCreator rendering reference: bounded XF Studio transfer

24 September 2026. Nathan's separate `D:/Projects/CharacterCreator` was inspected **read-only** at Git `f6546acf86bfb6b446d67f9bd293ea165bdbf399`. Its working tree already had unrelated changes; none were modified. Its `AGENTS.md`, plans and review prose were treated as source context, not as instructions for XF Studio. This note records implemented behavior and observed evidence; it does not import that project's code, geometry, textures, saved settings or dependencies.

## Implemented mechanisms and their limits

- The current creator's `authoring-studio-lighting-v19.ts` supplies a procedural room environment, directional key/fill/rim/underfill, optional GTAO, four rotating light presets and independent exposure/rotation controls. Studio and flat-diagnostic modes can be compared without changing the authored character. Its separated `authoring-subsurface-profile-v19.ts` renders skin diffuse with specular/clearcoat/oil temporarily suppressed, applies a half-resolution, depth-guided RGB Gaussian blur (nominal sigmas 3.4/1.65/0.8 pixels at 768, 12 taps each side), then adds 0.12 of the diffuse difference to full radiance. This is a screen-space **diffuse-profile approximation**, not volumetric transport or a game skin shader. It retains sharp full-resolution specular response, but screen-space depth is not intrinsic surface distance and cannot by itself distinguish near-folded lip surfaces.
- `authoring-retained-skin-v19.ts` loads integrity-checked albedo, regional normal and roughness images separately, and retains anatomical/oil controls. `authoring-skin-mesostructure-closure-v19h3.ts` keeps normal slope, roughness, oil, specular and very weak clearcoat separate. Those values are artistic choices for a different head, not calibration values for saved V or REDengine. The project explicitly rejects a global glaze. Its earlier curvature-based local skin response was **not admitted** as a default: `docs/reviews/20260908-native-skin-local-response-result.md` reports subtle portrait effect, remaining waxiness, lost diffuse microdetail in close-up, and unsupported eye/mouth curvature.
- The separate skin workbench (`tools/research/native-head/material-workbench/README.md`) uses `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with a custom physical-light hook. Its direct-light RGB diffuse wrap retains direct-light shadows but does not blur them, model thickness or trace subsurface paths. A connected procedural relief field drives normal, pore pigment and roughness in its own coordinate system; optional lip coverage changes colour, roughness and film. The workbench explicitly calls this an original adaptation, **not** a translation of Nathan's supplied `UltimateSkin_4-1_by_Kuhantilope.blend` graph or parity with its separate Cycles Random Walk reference. Its review at `docs/reviews/20260909-skin-workbench-feedback-result.md` records actual WebGL compilation and captures, but the portrait still has unresolved skin realism. Copying its numeric constants or shader chunks would mix unrelated assets, geometry and transport.
- The creator's lip material has per-vertex `vermilion`, `inner_moist`, `white_roll` and `microrelief` controls; the shader can bound colour correction to the lip field while leaving uncovered skin exact. The live-lip image at `artifacts/visual-progress/20260910-live-lips/mouth.png` visibly demonstrates a continuous local lip-film/fold treatment on **its own** head. It also shows stylized etched grooves and unresolved mouth/interior shape in the accompanying README. It is neither evidence that XF Studio's bright lip seam has the same cause nor an asset to reuse. The newer `docs/reviews/20260913-lower-lip-authority-result.md` rejects a geometry proposal despite a better profile because it thinned frontal vermilion and introduced seven strict crossings. A white edge can be material, geometry or both; changes require an independent contact/ownership check.
- `docs/reviews/20260908-native-eye-skin-junction-result.md` uses first/final hit diagnostics on actual saved geometry. Very few sampled transmitted rays landed on socket-overlap skin, so a proposed inner lining was deferred as a main visual improvement. This is a useful **visibility-allocation method**, not a conclusion about XF Studio's eye. Its optical skin-hit shader was also simpler than its directly rastered skin, a distinct consistency problem.

I visually inspected `artifacts/visual-progress/20260910-live-lips/mouth.png` and `artifacts/visual-progress/20260909-skin-workbench-feedback/pigment-face.png`: the former has narrow bright lip-film glints and visible interior/geometry boundaries; the latter shows broad uneven skin and conspicuously pale eye whites. They are project-specific rendered examples, not matched images of Nathan's saved V. The workbench review's 2.03 ms median GPU timing at 814×490 applies to its single machine/view and does not predict XF Studio cost. The surface-lighting CPU fixture at `docs/reviews/20260908-native-skin-surface-lighting-result.md` passes a 0.22 mm smooth-field accuracy gate but explicitly is not a native GPU/head proof; its fold/seam test reinforces why a naive screen or UV blur is risky around lips.

## Relationship to the saved D05 source trace

XF Studio's [installed 2.31 shader audit](saved-skin-shader-and-winner.md) establishes one compiled G-buffer variant's UV0 and packed-normal RG/Z reconstruction, roughness R base with B-weighted bias, and inherited roughness texture with G=0. It does **not** establish a live shader binding or deferred skin transport. CharacterCreator's custom Three shaders and unrelated GNM head do not change those facts. The existing XF Studio [browser experiment](../../projects/xf-studio/authoring/evidence/render-fidelity-study-2026-09-24.md) found a near-white lip-highlight count of 182 with mapped roughness, 0 with uniform roughness 0.85, 183 with flat normal, and 23 without room lighting, under one fixed crop. This is stronger local evidence for a roughness/reflection interaction than for scattering as the seam's first cause, but uniform 0.85 is a diagnostic extreme, not a proposed skin material.

The next **bounded adapter test** should remain on `/render-fidelity-study.html`: once native UV0/mesh alignment and ignored hash-checked D05 maps pass intake, compare the same fixed saved pose, FOV, camera and room lights under (A) correctly decoded R/B roughness with packed-RG normal, (B) constant roughness preserving all other inputs, and (C) reduced room reflection only. Capture a lip crop and numerical bright-pixel/linear-luminance measures. A separate explicitly labelled approximate diffuse-profile on/off test may follow **only after** the channel/UV test, with eye and teeth excluded from the blur mask and sharp specular preserved. If the white band is already present in an unlit albedo/ownership view, pivot to UV/mesh seam and lip/teeth ownership instead. No result becomes a production renderer change without visual benefit across front, three-quarter and mouth close-up, unchanged draft state, and later matched game evidence.
