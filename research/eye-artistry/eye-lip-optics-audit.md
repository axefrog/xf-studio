# Eye and lip optics: bounded source audit

24 September 2026. This is an offline diagnosis of the waxy saved eyes and bright upper/lower lip seam in Nathan's screenshots. It changes no renderer, game resource or saved character. The current game shader cache and material templates establish **inputs and compiled arithmetic**, not a visual match to REDengine under Nathan's lighting.

## Evidence that changes the eye plan

The saved eye remains Kala's `eye_16_diffuse` through Unique Eyes to CCXL; the [resolution](modded-eye-resolution.md) and [preview adapter](eye-preview-adapter-plan.md) retain the exact app-hash/definition and ignored texture hashes. The browser currently gives that 512² sRGB diffuse to one opaque `MeshStandardMaterial` with constant `roughness: 0.18`, no eye normal/roughness map, and a RoomEnvironment plus two directional lights (`authoring/src/scene.ts`, lines 44–62 and 99–146). The user-supplied close-up's “waxy” description is valid feedback, but the current source does not yet identify which optical term is responsible.

The **installed 2.31** `shader_final.cache` (SHA-256 `339145371a3b5aaa08eb4ef82d558f445b632e28603ee0f3b4860270dfc3ccfa`) contains 10 `eye` MeshSkinned variants. The G-buffer pixel program GUID `14845425953312192161` (DXBC SHA-256 `38cc7fb4b2800be113c1075e428eb609a49036c5b8a61ca008286612926f42d3`) provides stronger evidence than the prior Blender-adapter guess:

- The shader samples the packed normal's **R and G**, maps them to −1…1 and reconstructs Z with `sqrt(max(0, 1 − x² − y²))` (local disassembly `%220`–`%230`). It separately samples `NormalBubble` and combines another eye-specific normal/refraction construction (`%253`–`%343`). A plain PNG passed as Three's conventional RGB normal is wrong.
- The albedo and primary normal use a computed, view/eye-parameter-dependent UV (`%214`, `%215`). The roughness sample instead uses the original interpolated UV (`%17`, `%18`; `%359`). The preview's folded single-tile eye UV loses native side/tile information described in the prior adapter audit. This is direct evidence against assuming every eye map shares one unmodified UV path.
- The **R component** of the roughness sample (`%360`) is multiplied by material constant register 21 (`%362`) and written to G-buffer target 2, channel G (`%589`). The 2.31 `eye.mt` identifies register 21 as `RoughnessScale` (default `0.493420988`), and register 2 as `Roughness`. Thus the earlier suggestion to use green because Three/Blender normally uses green is **not the game eye shader's observed channel**. Kala's decoded roughness PNG is not channel-identical (R 8–132, G 4–134); a faithful source adapter should explicitly sample R and apply the resolved material scale, rather than silently accept Three's default G. This is specific to this `eye` compiled variant, not a universal REDengine texture rule.

The extracted 2.31 `eye.mt` is hash `7679860726048573814`, SHA-256 `c6eff77a473912680a5d3e2e439ef4fe6d9e664ee6238d0745e1e39b801e073e`. The inherited Unique Eyes material sets `NormalBubble`, `Blick`, iris/refraction/parallax and anti-lightbleed terms as detailed in the adapter audit. In the sampled Kala roughness PNG the median is R 25/255 (whole image) and R 82/255 in a 70×70 central pupil/iris window; multiplied by the template's ~0.493 scale these would be ~0.048 and ~0.159 **before filtering, UV mapping and deferred lighting**. They are not direct Three roughness settings or an argument to make the browser eye uniformly glossier. A single `0.18` cannot capture the map's spatial variation, but the game shader also changes normals and UVs. Do not diagnose “waxy” as simply excessive gloss without a controlled image comparison.

Reproduce the cache selection with `bun projects/xf-appearance-studio/authoring/tools/inspect-eye-skin-cache.ts`; it writes only ignored `research/consumers/eye-lip-shader/raw/` data. The selected DXBC was inspected with Windows SDK 10.0.22621 x64 `dxc.exe -dumpbin`. The reader follows the project's existing cache parser and verifies region bounds, stage headers and source hash. The compiled shader has anonymous constant buffers; the register-to-material identification above is checked against 2.31 serialized `eye.mt`, while the full lighting stage and actual runtime variant selection remain unproven.

## Why the lip seam is a separate problem

The preview head uses **one old Blender-master UV tile** for colour, normal and roughness, not the saved `skin_type_05` material chain. The source is `xfea_head2.blend` and `UDIM_d/n/r.png`; the exporter crops tile `(0,0)` and resizes it to 2048². The browser applies `MeshStandardMaterial` with `roughness: 0.85`, `roughnessMap`, `normalMap` and `normalScale=(0.35,-0.35)`, under the same high-energy environment/lights. Three's roughness map convention samples G and multiplies the scalar. The fixed texture/source hashes are in `authoring/evidence/asset-manifest.json`; current output hashes: colour `5f50adb99a385882ec162d1c03a29d1bd4e88011e43f06bc5c1161d99e06005a`, normal `416bbfc2db339058a371229310978852e9ec73c8a0cda0ae464522537a1b2e1f`, roughness `7b6353b22e82c250c4f598669e92a14e5f682007ca50525306cdf29b3f6b08c3`. The head GLB is `72b46566276bf87786d2b8025800278b41833194b45359792d380009bc3f82e8`.

The lip seam in Nathan's screenshot (SHA-256 `6b29b33eb1367a771619849b79a9d6e55f59a163b40fe111d9ca6e8ecf777f8`) is bright white. Direct inspection of the cropped colour tile finds painted mauve/dark lips, **not a matching white stroke**. In a broad 310×120-pixel lip-region rectangle of the roughness tile, all RGB channels are equal; median is 95/255 and range is 56–197/255. Through the browser's 0.85 multiplier the median nominal roughness is ~0.317, so that region is intentionally glossier than much of the surrounding skin. The rectangle is deliberately broad and not a per-pixel seam segmentation. This supports testing a lighting/specular cause, but does **not** establish that the photographed narrow white line is solely a roughness-map artifact; overlapping lip geometry, normal discontinuity, missing inner-mouth components and mismatched saved skin are still plausible.

The current 2.31 `base\materials\skin.mt` (resource hash `41843641457625498`, extracted SHA-256 `d7e50733acd1881805898078fd642a3fb9d70346e3ae23bcd4ebff4157ad4720`) declares `RMT_Subsurface`, `SkinProfile`, `MicroDetail`, roughness-detail biases and `renderstage_skin_translucency` techniques. The installed cache has 23 MeshSkinned `skin` variants, including translucency passes; selected G-buffer pixel GUID `12806642364631437234` has DXBC SHA-256 `8040f820f64055156d8223e65d1463e785ca62007db8eaae92356cadcfa36053`. The browser's standard material has none of these skin-specific terms. This proves that adding generic SSS or switching rendering backends alone cannot reproduce the saved face or diagnose the lip line. The effective saved skin instance, its texture paths and the game's mouth/interior components have **not** yet been resolved in this audit; `skin.mt` defaults are not a substitute for them.

## Prioritized corrections and acceptance gates

1. **Eye: resolve and stage, without changing the existing diffuse fallback.** Extend the ignored exact-choice manifest with Kala's normal and roughness PNG digests already recorded in the adapter audit. Build an isolated eye material experiment that reconstructs Z from RG and samples roughness **R**, with an explicit material scale. Test orientation/handedness on both eyes during gaze and at neutral pose. Compare a source-map on/off and map-channel A/B under one fixed camera and lights. Keep the game-specific bubble/refraction/anti-lightbleed path marked approximate until it is reproduced or bounded separately.
2. **Lip: isolate the white line before broad SSS work.** At the same saved morphs, idle phase, camera/FOV and light, capture (a) current preview, (b) no environment/specular, (c) roughness override, (d) flat normal, and (e) an interior/geometry wireframe-depth diagnostic. If (b)/(c) remove it, target roughness/light calibration; if (d) removes it, target tangent/normal handling; if it persists, inspect lip seam topology/inner-mouth assembly. The current source does not justify painting the line away in colour or arbitrarily raising all skin roughness.
3. **Saved skin chain:** resolve `skin_type_05` / `h0_000_pwa__basehead__01_ca_pale_00_warm_ivory` to the effective 2.31 `.app → .mesh/.mi → .mt/.sp/XBM` inputs, including installed mod overrides. Only then choose a bounded browser skin approximation and compare against a matched game capture. Record actual provider/winner separately from source candidates.

No game launch or distribution of extracted game/mod assets occurred. This report is source inspection plus a screenshot-based hypothesis; it is not runtime parity evidence.
