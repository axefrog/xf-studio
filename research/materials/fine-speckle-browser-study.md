# Denser fine-speckle browser Glitter candidate

24 September 2026, isolated `codex/glitter-fine-scale` worktree. This is an explicitly selected **browser preview**, not a verified cosmetic material or REDengine export. The shape, colour and light in the check differ from Nathan's [reference photographs](../backlog/glitter-visual-references.json), whose original photographers/makeup artists are not established. The photographs remain outside Git. Nathan's relayed explanation from island_dancer of `glitter_flakes_5.sbs`—triangle/square primitives with individually rotated axial gradients, seeded tiling, height-to-normal conversion and histogram-mapped light/dark colours—continues to inform the facet normal; no Substance graph or asset is copied.

## Version and method

`xfs/recipe-10`/`uv-cell-direct-3` is opt-in. Recipes 7–9 retain their raster, original direct and clustered looks. The new model retains deterministic UV anchoring, the triangular/quadrilateral polygon boundary, varying axial-height slope and base pigment. It places a high-density 2304-cell field of mostly tiny facets over a sparse 768-cell field of larger facets. The large field gets greater weight as the micro field becomes subpixel; both use finite 3×3 neighbourhood evaluation and fade to a low-energy mean when even the larger field is unresolved. Density remains an upper envelope modulated by a stable smooth cluster field, not a promise of visible particles. The model adds no optical-map bake, keeps the completed-mask worker/disposal path, and is still rejected by the game preset compiler. Switching into this brighter profile scales the current strength by about two thirds; Undo preserves the prior values.

The [reproducible candidate-centre tool](../../projects/xf-appearance-studio/authoring/tools/measure-direct-speckles.ts) samples the default Petal-wash mask at 1024, seed 2077, density .88 and fine share .88. Of 7,034 mask pixels above 50% alpha, the old clustered 1536 grid has 15,848 candidate centres inside the mask and 6,458 occupied; the new 2304 grid has 35,603 centres and 23,131 occupied, 20,302 in its fine population. The sparse 768 grid adds 775 occupied centres. Centre/mask and cluster sampling are approximations; none of these counts is a visible sparkle count. UV radii are .000042–.000082 for fine facets, .00010–.00017 for the less common coarse facets, and .00019–.00047 for the lower-frequency population. These are UV units, not millimetres or projected pixel sizes.

## Screen check and limits

The isolated `127.0.0.1:4442/?verify=1` view used one symmetric Petal wash over the neutral head, 1024 mask, `#592640` pigment, `#d8a0dd` facet colour, .95 density, .85 fine share and strength 6, with identical front camera/light and all other layers hidden. The new mode shows many small features at close-eye distance where recipe-9 remained scattered; at full-face distance some sparse larger flashes survive instead of the fine field disappearing entirely. In equal 225×150 and 235×150 close-eye screen rectangles, a +30/255 luminance difference against **each model's own strength-zero image** marked 939/718 pixels for recipe-9 and 3,129/2,402 for the new mode. The brighter pixels merge, so eight-connected components were 289/196 versus 219/283; component count alone would mischaracterise density. At full-face scale, equal 109×54 and 103×54 eye rectangles marked 212/85 pixels for recipe-9 and 262/228 for the new mode. Screenshot contrast is a fixed-camera display proxy, not a flake or BRDF measurement. Photographic highpass counts in the [earlier audit](../../experiments/007-irregular-glitter/visibility-audit.md) include eyelashes and imaging texture and cannot be equated with this on/off comparison.

The browser compiled the shader without console warnings/errors. At the close-eye view on this device, rolling 180-frame intervals were 17.5 ms median / 18.2 ms p95 for recipe-9 and 17.6 ms / 18.2 ms for recipe-10; CPU `renderer.render` calls were .4/.5 versus .4/.6 ms median/p95. These are not GPU timings or guarantees for slower devices. The stronger field can look like fine metallic frost, especially on the high outer Petal shape; the reference photos concentrate much of their sparkle on the mobile lid. Shape placement, photometric response, orbit stability, other lighting, visual acceptance and any REDengine mapping remain open. This change does not relax the Glitter game-export guard.
