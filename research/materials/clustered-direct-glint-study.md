# Clustered direct-light Glitter — isolated browser study

24 September 2026, `codex/glitter-clusters`. This is an **opt-in browser-only** continuation of the [direct-light checkpoint](direct-glint-browser-checkpoint.md), not a game material or a claim of photo matching. The local [reference manifest](../backlog/glitter-visual-references.json) supplied photographs 6 and 7 (SHA-256 `c77d4807…` and `d87d41fe…`) as visual targets; photographer and makeup-artist attribution remains unresolved. No photograph or game asset is added to this commit.

`xfs/recipe-9` stores `uv-cell-direct-2`. Recipe-8 `uv-cell-direct-1`, recipe-7 raster Glitter and older looks retain their original appearance. The Studio offers an explicit switch both ways. The newer shader modulates each UV-cell candidate's occupancy with a smooth, seeded 18-cell-scale field, reduces the largest polygon sizes, approximates subpixel area before lighting, widens angular response and extends the magnification-to-mean transition. It retains the existing mask worker and a continuous pigment sheen. The facet pattern is deterministic in UV and has no clock/screen-position input. The direct model still owns only the completed shape mask; at 2K the existing conservative generated-resource estimate is 91 MiB for one enabled layer. Glitter remains blocked by the flat game compiler.

Visual check used the isolated `127.0.0.1:4441/?verify=1` preview, a single symmetric Petal wash with `#592640` base, 90% density, 75% fine share, strength 10, the same front camera and light, surface guides off. Switching models preserved framing and controls. At close-eye scale, the original had large bright white/pink angular streaks at the lash line. The new model broke many of those into smaller points and gave the outer wash clustered specks. At full-face scale, extending the fade kept some distinct flecks visible instead of reducing the whole layer to satin-like sheen. The 1K and 2K clustered views looked similar at the same camera. These are qualitative screen observations, **not counted flakes**; the source eye shape and lighting differ from the photographs. The photographs still show substantially denser micro-sparkle across the central lid. The Studio remains too sparse there, and the remaining lash-line highlights can still look painted. Better reference framing and light-response calibration are needed before calling this visually accepted.

The live browser diagnostics reported one 2K mask, zero optical/albedo maps and no console errors. In this local in-app browser's rolling 180-frame window at the same 2K framing, clustered preview had 17.6 ms median / 18.2 ms p95 intervals and 0.4 / 0.6 ms CPU render-call durations; the original direct model had 17.5 / 18.2 ms and 0.3 / 0.5 ms respectively. These values are not GPU timings or a cross-device guarantee. `bun test` passed 228/228 after local ignored head assets were supplied; typecheck and browser build passed. The suite covers explicit recipe versioning, workspace/SQLite/collection round-trips, mask-only worker, material disposal and the export guard.
