# Code health ledger

The open code and design findings of Medium severity and above, from merge reviews and audits. Fixed findings are removed (git keeps their history); Low findings that are genuine bugs live as one-line items in the relevant [backlog](../backlog/README.md) file.

- **High:** a correctness bug, a broken or bypassed boundary, a security or data-loss risk, or a design that would force rework of later features.
- **Medium:** maintainability debt that will slow the next features.

**More than 3 open High findings pause feature merges** until the count is back to 3 or fewer; cleanup and critical-path fixes may still merge.
