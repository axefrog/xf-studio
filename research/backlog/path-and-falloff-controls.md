## Continuous point pigment checkpoint

Item 3 now has a production repair. `xfs/recipe-4` layers use a continuous boundary blend, avoiding the old nearest-segment strength switch. New layers default to smooth; existing v1–v3 layers retain exact legacy rendering until the author enables **Smooth point gradients**. That upgrade is undoable, survives browser/SQLite save and can coexist with unchanged legacy layers. **Selected point pigment** is separate from **Edge softness**; **Point blend** controls how nearby strength targets mix. Zero strength at a point is not promised to erase all pigment there because nearby targets blend.

Thin opposing gradients and very small blend footprints can still require more sampling; Bézier/corner/directional softness and the later gesture requests remain open.
