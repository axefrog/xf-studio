# Story Engine decisions

Every settled decision, in one place, so that nothing depends on a conversation being remembered. A decision is changed only by a new decision that replaces it here. Each row gives where it came from and where it is worked out.

**Sources:**
- **maintainer:** the project maintainer's direction;
- **agreement:** settled with another coordinating chat and recorded on the coordination board;
- **coordinator:** this vertical's own call within its remit, open to the maintainer's override.
