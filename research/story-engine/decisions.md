# Story Engine decisions

Every settled decision, in one place, so that nothing depends on a conversation being remembered. A decision is changed only by a new decision that replaces it here. Each row gives where it came from and where it is worked out.

**Sources:**
- **maintainer:** the project maintainer's direction;
- **agreement:** settled with another coordinating chat and recorded on the coordination board;
- **coordinator:** this vertical's own call within its remit, open to the maintainer's override.

| D-22 | 23 Oct 2026 | **NPCs may voice their own belief about V's motive.** A line marked `attribution` may have an NPC state what it believes V's reason was, if the NPC really holds that belief (a `believes` atom is yes), the belief rests on an act of V's that was observed, the line is phrased as the NPC's view, and V has a reply that can correct it. | D-23 | 23 Oct 2026 | **Author-fill seams.** Where the genre calls for legal material the project's model-driven tools may not write, the engine still handles that class fully: it sows, fits, casts, composes, places and times it, procedural seeds included, within the player's settings. Only the source material (text and assets) for such a class comes from packs people write; every such fragment has a non-explicit partner that leaves the same facts. Adult themes are part of the genre and get full design depth; nothing sexual involving minors, ever. Instructions: "allow for what you'd want to do and make it so that humans can fill in the gaps that you're not allowed to venture into"; and, on whether the engine itself can do it, "I assume this is just a 'what's in the data' thing" | maintainer | [S2 §2.19](spec/seeding.md#219-content-classes-ceilings-and-floors), [dossier 18](craft/d18-content-boundaries.md) |
| D-24 | 23 Oct 2026 | **Reading and analysing game data for modding is normal modding.** Extracting and analysing the game's files to build mods (the quest corpus, the facts table, knowledge pages, lint calibration on game text) continues, as across the modding community. The constraint is non-commercial: nothing is sold. Packs still refer to game content by id rather than redistributing it. This settles dossier 19's open question 2 on the User Agreement's text-and-data-mining clause. The maintainer adds that the upcoming game update 2.5 explicitly adds support for mods in the game. Quote: "The whole modding community routinely mines and mods the game data for the purpose of modding. CDPR endorses modfing, they just don't endorse charging for mods and the like." | maintainer | [dossier 19](craft/d19-rights-platforms.md) |
| D-25 | 25 Oct 2026 | **The protagonist is a parameter, not V.** A longer-term ambition for the whole project is a new protagonist who isn't V. The engine therefore treats "the player's character" as a configured actor: the specs' `v` is that actor's role, not V's identity or backstory. New design, specs and packs must not hard-wire V-specific lore (V's history, Johnny, the relic) into engine rules. Authored packs may still be written for V, as content. Existing V-named spec text is read as "the protagonist"; a later spec pass renames it where that helps. Quote: "Down-the-road ambition for the whole XF project overall is to investigate having an entirely new protagonist who isn't V. So from that perspective the focus on a V-centric storyline is probably more a distraction than anything." | maintainer | [S0](spec/kernel.md), [S2](spec/seeding.md) |
| D-26 | 25 Oct 2026 | **Demos centre on synthesised characters and situations.** Sample and demo scenarios mostly avoid the game's main characters, and feature generated people and new situations. The setting's institutions (the major gangs and corporations, the NCPD, districts and places) are used freely. A main character appears only where a test deliberately needs one to prove or harden an engine path, labelled as such. This is a content choice, not an engine rule: casting stays general. Quote: "general focus should be on synthesising new characters and situations for the most part. Broader setting-related stuff is fine, e.g. Tyger Claws are a major gang, Arasaka is a major corp." | maintainer | |
