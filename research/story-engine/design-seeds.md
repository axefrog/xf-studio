# Design seeds

Fine-grained design points that come up before the plan reaches their level. Each one waits here until a second-level plan absorbs it. That plan then owns the point, and the entry here links to where it went. Add a seed whenever one comes up. Plans are named in the [first-level plan](plan.md#5-second-level-plans).

Status: **waiting** (not yet absorbed), **anchored** (absorbed by a written plan, with a link), **resolved** (designed or decided), **dropped** (with the reason).

| DS-47 | **The world as sampled fields over dimensions.** A domain of concern is a bundle of dimensions: geography, the built environment (building, floor, street), organisations, weather, time, people, and so on. Each dimension's content is a field: a set of constants, a graph, a vector field, or control points with falloff. Sampling at a locality (a point or region across the dimensions) returns what is there, with every condition and context a seed can anchor to. The same idea as a voxel engine sampling a density function, with only sampled places ever materialised | [foundation](foundation.md), P3 | anchored |

| DS-70 | **A story is a cascade of causes; a pattern is a partial causal graph.** A pattern names junctures (events or states) and the relationships each must satisfy (who wronged whom, who owes, who saw, what this juncture caused), never a fixed sequence or cast. Fitting a juncture means finding something in the world that satisfies all its relationships: a theft, a betrayal or an unpaid debt can each fill "B wronged A". **One pattern, read at different points:** the junctures already matched are the past (what the sifter finds), the open ones are the future (what the director can wait for or nudge towards). Sifting and sowing become the same operation. **Composition falls out:** patterns join where one juncture's effect is another's cause, layer where they share junctures, and grow where an outcome opens a larger-scale pattern. It is the composite's grain (D-28), designed in the [pattern language](composite/pattern-language.md), and the route away from whole-plot templates. Costs to design: matching stays incremental (partial matches advanced as events arrive) and bounded by relevance; each cause must be legible to the player. Builds on DS-01, DS-02, DS-09 and DS-11, and on the sifter extras S3 deferred (telling before completion, composed patterns). **Template and formula are one structure at different levels of abstraction:** the more abstract, the more holes the engine fills from the simulation, and the more freely the pattern composes. **It is recursive in both directions:** a juncture can be filled by a single event or by a smaller pattern, which is opened only when it becomes relevant to the protagonist; patterns compose upwards into larger stories and refine downwards into tighter-scoped threads. This is the world's level-of-detail rule (D-07: no atoms, every locality can be opened) applied to story | P4, P5, WP16, [pattern language](composite/pattern-language.md) | resolved: composite v0, adopted into S0 v4.17, S1 v0.6, S2 v1.11 and S3 v0.6 |
| DS-71 | **Non-verbal vocal cues for unvoiced protagonist lines.** So that an unvoiced line (D-29) is not silent, the protagonist makes a short wordless sound from existing recordings ("huh", "heh", "hmm", "ugh", a sigh), in the voice the player chose. **The cue follows the act the player picked and the scene's register, not an inferred feeling:** a refusal in a tense scene, a question, a laugh at a joke. That keeps D-09: the engine reads the player's choice, not the protagonist's heart, and a wordless sound commits to little. Each cue is tagged with the register it suits, chosen least recently used and rate-limited so it never grates, and the player can turn cues off. Repurposing existing recordings is allowed (S2 §2.20). **Needs:** a catalogue of the protagonist's wordless recordings by register, for both voices (a research request), and the bridge playing a chosen recorded line for the player character (requirement V3: "no chosen lines" yet) | S2, S7, P8; runtime requirement V3 | waiting |
| DS-72 | **A dialogue choice is an act with parameters, not a line.** Because lines are rendered at run time, the protagonist's choice can be composed along several axes instead of picked from a fixed list: the **act** (ask, refuse, bargain, press, reassure), the **manner** (warm, cold, joking, threatening), the **subject** (any fact, person or event the protagonist knows, from the canon record), and the **stake** (what is offered, shown or revealed: money, an item, a piece of evidence). The engine records the act and its parameters; the words are its rendering (D-29), shown to the player before the line is said, so the option still shows the exact words picked (D-09). **The other side has its own model:** the NPC answers from what they know and believe, so they can lie, deflect or not know. **Level of detail for conversation:** the default surface stays a few options suited to the moment; depth (raising any known subject, presenting evidence, setting a manner for the whole exchange) opens for a player who digs. **Moves outside the menu count too:** silence, walking away, interrupting, paying, drawing a weapon or scanning someone are acts the scene reads. **The interface is open, not a list.** The game's own dialogue widget shows 2 to 4 listed options, but its ink UI system is flexible enough to build almost any way of expressing intent: a wheel where direction picks the act and distance from the centre the manner, subjects and evidence as cards played into the exchange, the scanner as the pointer (scan a person or object mid-conversation to make it the subject), a price dial for a bargain, cutting in while a line plays. Prefer forms native to the setting, fast with controller and keyboard alike, and consistent across every exchange. Too many axes at once paralyses, so each appears only when it matters | S2 §2.9, S7, P8; dossier 21 | waiting |
| DS-73 | **Everything the engine generates is explainable in space.** Holotable shows the engine's spatial influences as their real shapes: fields (heat, presence, pressure) as tinted zones and gradients, bounded regions of concern (regimes, refuges, cover, reserved areas, level of detail), points with reach and falloff (an incident's spread by size, a witness's perception range, relevance around the vantage), and vectors (aftershock spread, displacement, routine movement, rumours travelling along ties). Most of the causes are already recorded; v1 of the layers publishes reach, flows, gradients and placed-by links | D-31, D-33; Holotable layers v1 | waiting |
