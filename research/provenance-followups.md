No such asset is tracked or packaged today.
- **Game content** from CD PROJEKT RED has no redistribution permission; extracted and game-derived resources stay local.
- **Licence notices** if bundled or distributed: WolvenKit (GPL-3.0; separate redistribution review if its binary is ever bundled), Cyberpunk Blender Add-on (GPL-3.0-or-later), Three.js (MIT; required with the [adapted glitter study code](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/tools/glitter-study-material.ts) and with any distributed app), Electrobun (MIT, naming Blackboard Technologies Inc.) plus the exact build's Bun, Hutch and WebView2 notices, ArchiveXL, Codeware and TweakXL (their release ZIPs carry LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES files), Bun, SQLite, SciPy and NumPy, Pillow, Mermaid CLI and Blender where applicable.
