## Reuse permissions and notices to clear before distribution

These are not authorship gaps, but they must be resolved before any packaging, reuse or public release:

- **Mod asset or code reuse** is not permitted by credit or local preview use: nutboy (page requires permission for asset reuse/modification), Kala (credit, noncommercial and game-use conditions), eagul (author permission for reuse/modification), Arkhe, icxrus, Alliekat, MELUMINARY, island_dancer, NoraLee (page prohibits framework reuploads) and the KnowSo team's UV Texture Framework (separate permission/credit review). No such asset is tracked or packaged today.
- **Game content** from CD PROJEKT RED has no redistribution permission; extracted and game-derived resources stay local.
- **Licence notices** if bundled or distributed: WolvenKit (GPL-3.0; separate redistribution review if its binary is ever bundled), Cyberpunk Blender Add-on (GPL-3.0-or-later), Three.js (MIT; required with the [adapted glitter study code](../projects/xf-studio/authoring/tools/glitter-study-material.ts) and with any distributed app), Electrobun (MIT, naming Blackboard Technologies Inc.) plus the exact build's Bun, Hutch and WebView2 notices, ArchiveXL, Codeware and TweakXL (their release ZIPs carry LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES files), Bun, SQLite, SciPy and NumPy, Pillow, Mermaid CLI and Blender where applicable. This list is not a complete licence inventory.
