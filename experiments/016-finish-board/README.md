# Finish board: one-session in-game comparison candidate

**Status:** two runtime sessions held 25 September (creator screen, then photo mode; see [Runtime results](#runtime-results)); the follow-up candidate is [experiment 017](../017-plate-depth/README.md). The board builds and passes the independent verifier through the production pipeline (TypeScript builder, WolvenKit 9.0.1, game 2.31, built-in eye plate); the offline evidence below predates the 0.4 mm plate lift, and a rebuild with the lift also passes (archive `0bb0edc9…b345`). Since the plate-local UV window its flat and faceted boards export 2048 × 512 window maps; that rebuild passes too (archive 937,984 bytes, `76a8915f…409c`; before the window 839,680 bytes), and the Fresnel boards' textures are unchanged ([experiment 019](../019-uv-window/README.md#rebuilds)). The runtime results below were seen on head-UV builds.

This is an asset-free XF Studio collection, [`finish-board.collection.json`](finish-board.collection.json), generated by [`make-board.ts`](make-board.ts). Each preset is one choice in the **XF Eye Artistry** selector (plus Off) and puts finish patches side by side on the eyelids or under the eyes, so one prepared session can answer the open finish questions. Every patch uses the ordinary export routes described in the [finish designs](../../research/materials/finish-designs/README.md); nothing here is a special diagnostic resource.

## What each preset tests

Patches on the lids are three abutting stripes per eye, overlapping by half their edge softness so coverage stays continuous (separate small patches with gaps compressed badly in the small mips and failed the verifier's decoded-mip gate). All finish patches share one plum pigment (`#6d4a7e`), so only the finish differs. "Left" means the image-left eye in the UV map (u < 0.5); stripes are listed outer corner to inner corner.

| Preset | Route | Left lid | Right lid | Question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board 1 · flat finishes | flat `@preset` | Matte · Satin · Glossy | Metallic · Glossy · Matte | Do the flat roughness/metalness values read as distinct finishes? Is single-lobe Glossy clearly glossier than Satin without looking metallic? Coloured reflections on Metallic in bright and dim scenes. |
| Board 2 · shimmer | faceted `@faceted` | Satin control · fine Shimmer · coarse Shimmer (glitter proxy: 64 cells, full tilt) | Glossy control · fine Shimmer · Matte control | Do facets flash with the light close up and merge into a sheen at face distance? Is skin normal detail between facets unchanged (mode 1)? Do the flat controls look identical to Board 1 (faceted material does not disturb flat patches)? Does coarse Shimmer read as glitter? |
| Board 3 · colour shift | `@fresnel_…3` | Duochrome lid, both eyes: base `#3a2350`, shift `#3fd4c2`, strength 0.8 | (mirrored) | Does the teal appear toward the lid edges and follow the **camera**, not the light? |
| Board 4 · shift control | `@fresnel_…4` | Same shape and base, strength 0 | (mirrored) | Control for Board 3: the gradient-recolour template alone. |
| Board 5 · blend steps | flat | Black Matte at 25 % / 50 % / 75 % opacity, under the eye | (mirrored) | Does decal colour blend in square-root space? Measured coverage 0.252 / 0.504 / 0.751. |
| Board 6 · metal ramp | flat | Satin base + Metallic ramp: metalness 0.02 → 0.29 across the lid | Satin base + five steps: metalness 0.051, 0.098, 0.149, 0.200, 0.302 | Is there a visible seam where metalness crosses 0.1 on skin (the Subsurface class leaves its SSS albedo path)? Roughness drifts only from 0.38 to 0.33 across the ramp. |

Predictions for Board 5 on flat-lit skin of linear luminance L: square-root-space blending gives L·(1 − a)² = 0.56 L, 0.25 L, 0.06 L; linear blending would give 0.75 L, 0.5 L, 0.25 L. The Matte patch also replaces skin roughness (0.88), so measure under soft, frontal light.

## In-game test card

Before the session: stage this candidate through the normal diagnostic transfer (it is **not** installed), confirm ArchiveXL 1.27.3 actually loads, and use female V with the default head. Fixed photo-mode camera and FOV, one key light you can rotate; record game and framework versions and keep the ArchiveXL log.

1. **Selector.** The XF Eye Artistry selector lists Off plus six boards; switching Board 1 → 2 → 3 → Off clears each look. Check the ArchiveXL log for material expansion errors (`@faceted`, `@fresnel_…`).
2. **Board 1** (close, then face framing): light sweep with a fixed camera, then camera orbit with fixed light. Glossy highlight sharper and smaller than Satin; Metallic tinted and darker in dim light.
3. **Board 2** (close photo mode): light sweep. Points flash on both Shimmer stripes, Satin control stays smooth, skin texture between facets unchanged; coarse stripe shows distinct flakes. Pull back to face framing: fine Shimmer ≈ a slightly broader, brighter Satin. Compare the flat controls with Board 1.
4. **Boards 3 and 4**: fixed light, orbit front to profile. Board 3 lid edges turn teal and the teal follows the camera; Board 4 stays plum. Then fixed camera, light sweep: the teal should not move with the light.
5. **Board 5** (soft frontal light, fixed exposure): screenshot for luminance measurement of the three steps against bare cheek.
6. **Board 6**: soft side light. Look for a visible seam near metalness 0.1 on the ramp (left lid) and compare with the second step (0.098) and third (0.149) on the right lid.
7. Blink and a slow head turn on Boards 2 and 3 (eyelid contact, normal composition during deformation).

Send the screenshots, a note per item, the ArchiveXL log and the versions. The finish pages say what each outcome means.

## Runtime results

**25 September 2026, first session** (female V, reference character, MO2 profile `XF Studio diagnostic 2026-09-25`, ArchiveXL log captured privately). Six creator-screen screenshots are held privately; no lighting control in the creator.

- **Selector:** present in the character creator with Off plus all six boards; every board rendered on the lids or under the eyes in the right place, and ArchiveXL instantiated every material (`@preset`, `@faceted`, both `@fresnel_…`) without errors or warnings.
- **Board 3 vs 4:** Board 3 shows teal toward the lid edges while Board 4 (strength 0) stays plum, consistent with the Fresnel route; camera dependence not yet tested.
- **Board 5:** the three under-eye steps read as clearly distinct densities; not yet measured.
- **Photo mode:** the makeup did **not** appear. The build listed the selector only in the `character_customization` head group; vanilla eye makeup's appearance options are also in `face`, which the player entities' face controller reads. The candidate was rebuilt with both groups (the verifier now requires them) and restaged.
- **Label overlap:** with the label "XF Eye Artistry", preset names of about 28 characters or more overlapped the label. The label is now "XF" and the board names are shorter.

Rebuilt candidate: archive SHA-256 `e77fd580…798b8b` (`.archive.xl` unchanged, `75ff8bbe…c556`), 6 presets named "Board 1 · flat finishes" … "Board 6 · metal ramp".

**25 September 2026, photo-mode session** with that rebuilt candidate (private screenshots `5.png`–`9.png`; the ArchiveXL log showed every material instantiated without errors):

- **Close-up breakup.** The makeup is clean at normal and medium distance and breaks into angular, skin-coloured patches close up (same preset at three distances). Cause: the plate coincided with the skin, where vanilla face decals sit 0.40 mm above it; fixed by the packaging lift in [experiment 017](../017-plate-depth/README.md).
- **Board 1** reads uniformly glossy, "almost plastic-like"; Matte and Satin do not read as matte or satin. The roughness does reach skin lighting; three of the four finishes are glossier than bare lid skin, and the breakup mixed in skin (experiment 017, question 2).
- **Board 2** Shimmer reads as a diffused gloss, not sparkle.
- **Boards 3 and 4:** the colour shift works: teal/purple changes with the camera angle, and the control stays plum.
- **Board 5:** the three under-eye steps are clearly distinct.
- **Board 6:** the ramp and steps show angular highlight shapes; whether there is a seam is unclear.
