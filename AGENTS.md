## Community provenance and credit — mandatory

Whenever a community mod, repository, tool, guide, discussion or individual teaches us something that informs research, implementation or design, add or update an entry in [the community credits and learning record](docs/community-credits.md) during that work, before its checkpoint. This applies to ideas and techniques even when no code or assets are copied. Record the project/mod name, author or contributors as evidenced (never guess), a source link, the version/commit or date when available, a brief concrete explanation of its value, and a link to our affected research/implementation. Distinguish learning/inspiration, tool/dependency use, code adaptation and asset reuse. Preserve upstream attribution and note applicable license/permission evidence for actual reuse; acknowledgement is not a substitute for permission or required notices. If authorship or provenance is unresolved, explicitly record the gap and follow it up rather than omitting the contribution. Update existing entries as new lessons accumulate. Before distribution, use this record to prepare visible acknowledgements and required third-party notices. An inventory listing alone is not a learning credit.
