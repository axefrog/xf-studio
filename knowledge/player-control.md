# Player control: moving V, the camera, interactions and input

**Maturity: Draft.** What the game offers a mod for driving V directly: teleporting and walking her, turning the camera instantly or smoothly, reading and choosing interaction and dialogue choices, the other things a player does (crouch, weapons, consumables, menus), and how input reaches the game and could be synthesised from inside it. Consolidated on 29 September 2026 from the decompiled 2.31 script bundle (redscript-cli 0.5.31, `final.redscripts` SHA-256 `2119046f…ee86`), the game's input configuration and executable imports, the address library, the `red-dump-json` RTTI dump (`a8e52990`, pre-2.3), RED4ext.SDK (`ad727771`), Cyber Engine Tweaks (`v1.37.1-9`), Codeware (clone 1.20.4, installed 1.20.5), Appearance Menu Mod, Input Loader and Native Interactions Framework clones, and installed mods read in place (FreeFly 2.4.0, Immersive Third Person - Best Of Both Worlds 1.3.0, 0-Engine 0.13.0). **Nothing on this page has runtime evidence yet** except where marked. The command design built on it is the [player-control design](../research/runtime/player-control-design.md); `experiments/player-control/probe_offline.py` reproduces the import, address-library and RTTI facts. Grades follow the [knowledge rules](README.md).

Game-script citations are paths inside the decompiled bundle; installed-mod citations are paths inside each mod's folder in the mod manager (`mods/<mod name>/`).

## 2. Moving V

- **Teleport.** `TeleportationFacility` has `Teleport(object, position: Vector4, orientation: EulerAngles)` and `TeleportToNode(object, NodeRef)`, nothing else [source] `orphans.script:31933-31938`. Fast travel calls `GetFPPCameraComponent().ResetPitch()` and then `TeleportToNode` [source] `core/systems/fastTravelSystem.script:321-341`. Appearance Menu Mod teleports V with `EulerAngles.new(0, 0, yaw)` and pins her by teleporting her onto her own position [source] AMM `Modules/tools.lua:1782-1786`, `Modules/util.lua:516-523`.
- **Teleport checks nothing.** Snap a destination with `FindPointInSphereOnlyHumanNavmesh(center, radius, NavGenAgentSize.Human, heightDetail)` and require `IsNavmeshStreamedInLocation`; the game's own `GetNearestNavmeshPointBelowOnlyHumanNavmesh` walks spheres downwards, and `IsOnGround` casts 0.2 m down against static geometry [source] `core/systems/navigationSystem.script:8, 18, 24-60`.
- **Paths.** `CalculatePathOnlyHumanNavmesh(start, end, agentSize, findPointTolerance)` returns a `NavigationPath` with `path: [Vector4]` and `CalculateLength()` [source] `navigationSystem.script:4`, `orphans.script:29743-29748`; an area-effect device uses it to test reachability [source] `cyberpunk/devices/aoeSystem/aoeArea.script:236`.
- **Walking is input.** The player state machine (PSM) reads the movement actions (`GetActionValue(n"MoveX")`, `n"MoveY"`, `GetInputHeading()`, `IsMoveInputConsiderable()`) to choose its states; the motion itself is native [source] `cyberpunk/player/psm/defaultTransition.script:587-588, 639`, `orphans.script:42516-42572`. `MoveX`/`MoveY` map to the left stick and the WASD buttons [resource] `r6/config/inputContexts.xml:895-896`, `inputUserMappings.xml`.
- **The PSM's own nudge.** `AdjustTransformWithDurations` (position, slide duration, rotation, rotation duration, target and distance radius, gravity, curve, parabolic motion), set as the temporary parameter `adjustTransform` on the PSM's `StateContext`, moves or turns V over time; melee lunges, takedown alignment and turning a knocked-down V use it [source] `orphans.script:42776-42798`, `defaultTransition.script:319-345`, `locomotionTransitions.script:5112-5121`. Only PSM callbacks receive a `StateContext`; Immersive Third Person turns V's body this way from its wrap of `StandEvents.OnTick` [source] ITP `r6/scripts/immersive_third_person/tpp_camera_bridge.reds:2030-2057`. `LocomotionParameters.SetUseCameraHeadingForMovement` (through the `locomotionParameters` temporary parameter) is how that mod makes movement follow the camera [source] `orphans.script:45147-45185`; ITP `tpp_camera_move360.reds`.
- **Noclip.** FreeFly applies `GameplayRestriction.NoMovement` and `NoZooming` (and optionally `NoCombat`) to V, then teleports her every frame along the active camera's forward and right vectors, turning her by the teleport's yaw plus the mouse's `CameraMouseX` [source] FreeFly `bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/freefly/modules/utils/logic.lua`, `miscUtils.lua`.
- **State to check first.** The PSM blackboard (`PlayerPuppet.GetPlayerStateMachineBlackboard()`) carries `Locomotion` (`gamePSMLocomotionStates`: Default, Crouch, Sprint, Jump, Vault, Dodge, Workspot, Slide…), `HighLevel` (Default, SceneTier1-5, Swimming), `SceneTier`, `Vehicle`, `Combat`, `Fall`, `Landing`, `Swimming`, `IsInWorkspot`, `Takedown`, `BodyCarrying` and more [source] `core/blackboard/blackboardDefinitions.script` (`PlayerStateMachineDef`), `orphans.script:6647-6693`; 0-Engine reads all of them every frame for its state cache [source] 0-Engine `modules/BlackboardCache2.lua`. Also `VehicleComponent.IsMountedToVehicle(game, player)` [source] `core/components/scriptComponents/vehicleComponent.script:255` and `SceneSystemInterface.IsEntityInScene/IsEntityInDialogue` [source] `orphans.script:32248-32252`.

## 3. The camera

- **Reading the look direction:** `CameraSystem.GetActiveCameraForward()` (pitch = `asin(z)`), `GetActiveCameraWorldTransform()`; V's facing `GetWorldYaw()` [source] `core/entity/entity.script:48`.
- **`TargetingSystem.LookAt(instigator, out AimRequest)`** turns the player's camera in yaw and pitch towards `lookAtTarget` over `duration`, with `maxDuration`, `easeIn`/`easeOut`, `precision`, `adjustYaw`/`adjustPitch`, `endOnTargetReached`, `endOnTimeExceeded`, `endOnCameraInputApplied`, `cameraInputMagToBreak`, `cameraMouseInputMagToBreak`, `processAsInput` and body-part tracking fields; `BreakLookAt`, `BreakAimSnap` end it [source] `orphans.script:22401-22423, 26044-26088`. The scanner's target switch (0.1 s) [source] `core/systems/hud/hudManager.script:640-670`, melee [source] `defaultTransition.script:720-755`, an item action that makes V look at a thrown knife [source] `cyberpunk/items/combat_gadgets/kurtThrowableKnife.script:295-313` and an effect executor (0.25 s, eased) [source] `cyberpunk/strike/executors/executorLookAt.script` call it, the last two from outside the PSM.
- **The first-person camera** is the player's `camera` component (`GetFPPCameraComponent()`, a `gameFPPCameraComponent`): `ResetPitch()`, `SceneDisableBlendingToStaticPosition()`, the placed-component `Get/SetLocalPosition` and `Get/SetLocalOrientation`, and the fields `pitchMin`, `pitchMax`, `yawMaxLeft`, `yawMaxRight`, `headingLocked`, `sensitivityMultX/Y` [source] `orphans.script:16646-16664, 27006-27011`; SDK `game/FPPCameraComponent.hpp`. The current pitch is in an unnamed field. Native Interactions Framework pulls this camera 2 m behind V (`SetLocalPosition(0, -2, 0)`) and widens its limits for an external view [source] NIF `modules/ui/interactionUI.lua:38-62`. Whether a local-orientation write sets pitch or is overwritten each frame is [hypothesis].
- **Hidden test natives:** `PlayerFunctionalTests.SetCameraOrientation` and `RemoveCollision` exist in retail RTTI, parameters stripped (§6) [source] `red-dump-json` `classes/PlayerFunctionalTests.json`.
- **Immersive Third Person** activates its own camera component (`tppCamera`) and sets its local orientation and position every frame from an orbit fed by the player's mouse and stick actions caught in `PlayerPuppet.OnAction`; it locks the first-person camera's heading while free-looking and resets it (`ResetPitch`, neutral orientation, origin) when switching back [source] ITP `bin/x64/plugins/cyber_engine_tweaks/mods/immersive_third_person/init.lua:1029-1070, 3890-3920, 4490-4600`. Its RED4ext plugin `ImmersiveThirdPersonMove360` swaps the native handlers of `StateGameScriptInterface.GetActionValue` and `GetInputHeading`, guarded against a table it can't verify, so the PSM reads the movement input it wants [source] the plugin's log strings. A third-person look is therefore that mod's own state, not the game's.
- **A free camera of one's own:** spawn `base\entities\cameras\simple_free_camera.ent`, `FindComponentByName("camera")`, `Activate(blendTime, false)`, `SetFOV`, `SetZoom`, move the entity with `Teleport`; `Deactivate` and `GetFPPCameraComponent().Activate()` to return. AMM holds V still meanwhile with `NoMovement`, `NoCameraControl`, `NoZooming`, `FastForwardCrouchLock`, `NoCombat`, `VehicleNoSummoning`, `NoPhone` and `NoDriving`, and shows her head [source] AMM `Modules/camera.lua`, `Modules/util.lua:541-561`.

## 4. Interactions and dialogue

- **Everything the HUD offers is on the `UIInteractions` blackboard** (global, auto-created): `InteractionChoiceHub` (`InteractionChoiceHubData { id, flags, active, title, choices }`), `DialogChoiceHubs` (`{ choiceHubs: [ListChoiceHubData { id, activityState, flags, isPhoneLockActive, title, choices: [ListChoiceData] }] }`), `ActiveChoiceHubID`, `SelectedIndex`, `LootData`, `ContactsData`, `ActiveInteractions`, `InteractionSkillCheckHub`, `NameplateOwnerID`, `VisualizersInfo`, `LastAttemptedChoice`, `LookAtTargetVisualizerID`, `HasScrollableInteraction` [source] `core/blackboard/blackboardDefinitions.script:805-838`; `orphans.script:42008-42026, 54489-54552`. The HUD listens to exactly these [source] `cyberpunk/UI/interactions/interactionUIBase.script:30-107`.
- **A world choice** (`InteractionChoiceData`) carries the `inputAction` that selects it (`Choice1`…`Choice4` or a hold), `isHoldAction`, a LocKey `localizedName` (shown with `GetLocalizedText`), a type, caption parts and `data` variants [source] `orphans.script:54537-54552`, `cyberpunk/UI/interactions/interactionsUIChoiceItem.script:64, 152`. **A dialogue line** (`ListChoiceData`) carries its text, type, caption parts and `inputActionName`, its dedicated input where it has one [source] `cyberpunk/UI/interactions/dialogUI.script:312-324`.
- **`LastAttemptedChoice`** (`InteractionAttemptedChoice { visualizerType, choice, choiceIdx, isSuccess }`) records the last choice made and whether it succeeded; the prevention system listens to it [source] `orphans.script:33653-33662`, `core/systems/preventionSystem.script:1150`.
- **Selection is native and input-driven.** The interaction manager turns the choice's action into an `InteractionChoiceEvent { hotspot, activator, layerData, choice: InteractionChoice { caption, captionParts, data, choiceMetaData }, actionType }` on the hotspot's entity [source] `orphans.script:18261-18268, 22133-22144, 35785-35790`. The script API of `InteractionManager` has only `IsInteractionLookAtTarget`, `SetBlockAllInteractions` and `AreSceneInteractionsBlocked` [source] `orphans.script:33671-33678`, and `SceneSystemInterface` has no choice function [source] `orphans.script:32199-32252`. Selecting a dialogue line therefore means input: `ChoiceScrollUp/Down` until `SelectedIndex` matches, then `DialogConfirm` (`IK_F`, `IK_Enter`, pad X) or the line's own input [resource] `r6/config/inputUserMappings.xml:1175-1200`.
- **Devices** handle the event with `OnInteractionUsed(evt) → ExecuteAction(evt.choice, evt.activator, evt.layerData.tag)` [source] `cyberpunk/devices/core/interactiveDevice.script:276-278`; containers, mountable vehicles and puppets and the inventory component have their own handlers [source] `core/components/lootContainers.script:113`, `gameVehicleMountableComponent.script:4-12`, `inventoryComponent.script:44`. Queuing such an event from script, without input, is [hypothesis].
- **Usable spots added by Native Interactions Framework are scene interactions** (it registers scene resources), so they select like dialogue [source] NIF `r6/scripts/nativeInteractions.reds`.

## 6. How input reaches the game, and synthetic input

- **Devices:** the executable imports `RegisterRawInputDevices` and `GetRawInputData` (keyboard and mouse as Raw Input), `XInputGetState` from `XINPUT9_1_0.dll`, `HID.DLL` functions (other pads), and `GetForegroundWindow`, `ClipCursor`, `SetCursorPos` [resource] 2.31 executable imports. Posted key messages never reach it and `SendInput` works only with the game in front [runtime] sessions 4 and 5 ([photo mode §2.3](photo-mode.md#23-routes-to-the-full-photo-mode)).
- **Where it can be seen:** the address library names `input::InputSystemWin32Base::Update` (hash `261693736`) [resource]; CET uses `input::InputSystemWin32Base::ForceCursor` (`2130646213`) and blocks game input by swallowing mouse, keyboard and `WM_INPUT` messages in its window-procedure hook [source] CET `src/reverse/Addresses.h:62-63`, `src/d3d12/D3D12.cpp:27-50`. Codeware reads decoded input by hooking `inkSystem::ProcessInputEvents` (hash `673454961`), whose `RawInputBuffer` holds 0x40-byte `RawInputData { EInputKey key, EInputAction action, float value, mouseX, mouseY, … }` records [source] Codeware `src/App/Callback/Controllers/RawInputHook.hpp`, `src/Red/Input.hpp`.
- **Actions are data.** Each action in `inputContexts.xml` maps to a mapping in `inputUserMappings.xml` listing keys and pad buttons, for example `Choice1` = `IK_F` or pad X, `TogglePhotoMode` = `IK_N` or both sticks clicked (`IK_PAD_LR_THUMB`) [resource] `inputUserMappings.xml:1175-1200, 2066-2069`. Input Loader adds actions by merging `r6/input/*.xml` (or a plugin's file) into copies in `r6/cache` and pointing the game at them with `engine/config/platform/pc/input_loader.ini`; it fires nothing [source] Input Loader `src/Main.cpp`. Mods consume actions with `RegisterInputListener` or by observing `PlayerPuppet.OnAction`.
- **CDPR's functional-test system is in the retail build.** `FunctionalTestsGameSystem` lists 60 native functions, among them `FakeInputPressAction`, `FakeInputReleaseAction`, `FakeInputHoldAction`, `FakeInputClickAction`, `FakeInputMultitapAction`, `FakeInputAxisAction`, `FakeInputReleaseAxisAction`, `FakePressButton`, `FakeReleaseButton`, `FakeSetAxis`, `TakeOverInput`, `ReleaseAllInput`, `LookAtPosition`, `NavigateFlatTowards`, `TeleportPlayer`, `GetPlayerPosition` and `GetPlayerOrientation`; `NavigationFunctionalTests` has `GetPathOnNavmesh`, and `UIFunctionalTests` has `Play`, `IsPlaying` and `GetPlaybackResult` [source] `red-dump-json` `classes/*FunctionalTests*.json`. **None has parameters in RTTI** (Codeware re-declares them empty) [source] Codeware 1.20.5 `Scripts/Codeware.Global.reds:16732-16791`. **The retail game doesn't register the system**; CET creates it as a singleton for its entity spawner, and adds the missing parameters of `WorldFunctionalTests.SpawnEntity` itself before calling that native's retail body, kept for mods that still call it [source] CET `src/reverse/RTTIExtender.cpp:341-390, 585-625, 660-667`. So the fake-input natives exist and are reachable with CET installed; whether their bodies do anything outside the test engine, and with which parameters, is [hypothesis]. Recovering a signature means reading the native handler's parameter reads in the executable, the method [game crashes §2.1](game-crashes.md#21-calling-scripts-from-native-code) used.
- **Other channels** [hypothesis]: a virtual XInput pad (replace the `XInputGetState` import in-process and merge a synthetic state with the real pad's; unknown whether the game polls pads in the background, and it switches its prompts to pad glyphs), or appending records to the decoded buffer before `inkSystem::ProcessInputEvents` (unknown whether gameplay reads the same buffer).

## Open questions

1. Do the `FakeInput*` natives work in the retail engine, with which parameters, and without window focus? A read-only probe of their handler offsets, then offline disassembly, then one supervised call ([design §2.5](../research/runtime/player-control-design.md#25-synthetic-input)).
2. Does the game poll XInput while its window is not in front?
3. Does gameplay input read the same decoded buffer that `inkSystem::ProcessInputEvents` receives?
4. Can script build an `InteractionChoiceEvent` that a device accepts from the hub's choice data?
5. Is the first-person camera's pitch settable directly, or only through `LookAt`?
6. Does a per-tick teleport ("glide") play any locomotion animation on V's third-person body, and does it trip area triggers as walking does?
